## Principles
- A deadline can usually be asked about politely. Asking for more time is common and rarely harms.
- Tell other employers about an offer honestly when it might speed their process.
- Never accept and then back out lightly; a withdrawn acceptance can damage reputation.
## Steps
- Ask the employer for time to decide, with a specific date and a reason.
- Tell the other employers you have an offer with a deadline and ask whether they can move faster.
- Compare the offers on the five things that matter.
- Decide by the date, and tell each employer your answer kindly, with thanks.
- Keep notes on every promise made.
## Ask
- Ask for the deadlines and which employers are still in process.
## Do not claim
- Do not state how long employers wait or give legal advice about offers.
