Bill payment needs more than a debit call. [Bank of America's Bill Pay help](https://info.bankofamerica.com/en/digital-banking/online-bill-pay) distinguishes scheduled confirmation, eligible eBills, recurring scheduling, electronic delivery and mailed checks. It describes different balance-debit timing for different delivery/account cases. Upmore must bind delivery timing and reversibility before offering a payment review. An immediate confirmation cannot mean the creditor credited the bill. Those terms remain an Upmore contract gap.

[Wells Fargo Bill Pay](https://www.wellsfargo.com/mobile-online-banking/bill-pay/) distinguishes history, reminders, scheduling, variable eBills and account balance effects. A service without a monthly Bill Pay fee can still affect overdraft exposure and other account fees. Upmore's empty execution registry prevents representing these partner services as operational.

[Propel's security page](https://www.propel.app/snap/propel-ebt-security-features/) visibly updated May 14, 2026 lists different regions for card locks, out-of-state blocking, online blocking and monitoring. Balance access does not establish access to any security write. Keep capability discovery and authorization separate for every state/account.

[Venmo's credit page](https://venmo.com/about/creditcard) includes daily rewards settlement and different monthly wording for cash-back-to-crypto. Resolve this against the program's current terms before modeling settlement or conversion schedules. Its application disclosures and device restrictions remain separate from ordinary payment access.

[Freecash cashout](https://freecash.com/en/cashout) explicitly labels methods region-dependent. Coins and withdrawal thresholds are not confirmed paid cash. Marketing reviews on this page are labeled unverified; they cannot establish realized earning outcomes.

[Google Wallet help](https://support.google.com/wallet/answer/11951709?hl=en) separates Wallet storage and Google Pay payments and limits recent activity; it directs users to bank evidence for full history. Local pass organization cannot establish tokenization or identity certification.

## Subscription and earning contract expansion

Nine subscription atoms separate recurrence hypotheses, expected charge details, billing authority, issuer block scope/lifetime, cancellation preparation, supported cancellation, subsequent billing verification and independent contract/block state. Nine earning atoms separate offer review, user-reported completion, provider attribution, reward accounting, withdrawal readiness, holds, received payouts and dispute preparation/submission. They are specifications or partner-blocked requirements, with no assigned implementation or test evidence. Empty implementation/test fields deliberately prevent claiming parity from a catalog alone.

[Capital One's subscription-specific help](https://www.capitalone.com/digital/tools/subscription-management/) adds material limitations beyond the app page: merchant blocks can affect other recurring charges, expire or be overridden. Selected-merchant cancellation requests use Minna Technologies; charges can continue pending cancellation and cancellation fees can apply. Those capabilities need independent issuer/vendor adapters, approval scopes and evidence.

[Freecash's uncredited-offer guide](https://freecash.com/academy/en/support/offer/tracking/resolving-uncredited-offers) directs support by originating provider and checks actual completion deadlines. Its completion-based waiting language differs from the beginning-based wording in [the ticket creation guide](https://freecash.com/academy/en/support/general/contact/how-to-create-a-support-ticket). The Upmore specification requires current offer/ticket eligibility readback before sending; no universal remembered wait constant.

[Held-offer help](https://freecash.com/academy/en/support/offer/miscellaneous/what-are-held-offers) distinguishes pending and withdrawable rewards and can omit a release time. [First-withdrawal help](https://freecash.com/academy/en/support/withdrawal/issues/first-cashout-requirements) distinguishes verification and region requirements from a later payout claim. Upmore cannot count a task report, approved reward or claim email as received money. [Ticket rejection help](https://freecash.com/academy/en/support/withdrawal/issues/why-ticket-rejected) also limits re-review: it is provider-discretionary, rather than a guaranteed appeal an agent can repeatedly request.
