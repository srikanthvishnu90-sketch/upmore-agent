# Product dependencies and owner decisions

Engineering launch gates, not a legal determination. Provider marketing and the supplied blueprint are not contracts. No launch clearance is inferred from a passing local test. Production effects remain disabled where the named adapter/permission is absent.

| ID | Product / jurisdiction | Required dependency | Current proof | Owner item / launch switch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | US bank aggregation | Supported institution/product, hosted authorization, granular reads, refresh/revocation, privacy/retention | Owner secret namespace and normalized read contracts tested locally; new live coverage unverified | Confirm pilot institution/account/product coverage and retention; read-only connection gate |
| D02 | US bills, ACH, transfers, wires, P2P/Zelle | Commercial/network authorization; exact payee/source; legal/security classification; fees, returns, error resolution, status readback | Payment/biller registries empty; durable synthetic tests only | Choose and approve supported rail, scope and legal review; verified adapter plus server approval/preflight gate |
| D03 | Custody, savings interest, debit/virtual cards, overdraft | Actual sponsor/issuer, account eligibility, servicing, product protection disclosures | No issuing/custody adapter | Partner agreement and product/jurisdiction review; production capability disabled |
| D04 | Check/cash deposits | Eligible bank/processor, endorsements, limits, holds, funds availability, deposit status | No deposit adapter | Approved processor and eligible accounts; production capability disabled |
| D05 | Credit, BNPL, financing, hard inquiry | Actual lender/issuer/bureau; exact inquiry/terms consent; underwriting and servicing review | No lender/bureau execution adapter | Define product and obtain legal/provider review; no automatic application or loan approval |
| D06 | Public investing, retirement, personalized advice | Broker/custodian/adviser operating roles, order/data permissions, fees, partial fills and tax lot coverage | Research/catalog and local planning; no newly verified broker execution | Owner selects advice versus education scope; agreements and separate launch approval; no live trade grant |

| D11 | Subscription cancellation | Correct vendor/purchase channel, permission, fees, effective date, receipt and later-billing checks | Preparation/catalog; no live cancellation adapter verified | One permitted vendor pilot; production effect needs exact approval |

Pending owner decisions do not block local research, deterministic analysis, user-entered records or explicitly labeled preparation. Do not ask for passwords, tokens or private financial documents in a plain-text prompt. Credentials belong in the approved provider-hosted/secret-manager flow.
