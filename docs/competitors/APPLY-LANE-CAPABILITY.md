# Apply lane: capability vs Tsenta and Simplify (2026-10-21)

The owner's headline is capability, not trust. This page compares what each product's apply lane can do. It does not
compare how safe each one feels.

## Labels and method
- **Upmore column.** Read from the code on `wo/first-real-submission` at 515d48f2, not from the docs. Three tiers per cell:
  - **Today**: what the shipped defaults do (`extension/config.js`: `personSubmits: false`, `agentSubmits: false`).
  - **Off**: built and tested locally, but behind a flag that is off.
  - **Next**: not built.
  The extension is loaded unpacked as a test build; it is not in the Chrome Web Store. Real submissions to date: **0**.
- **Competitor cells.**
  - **VERIFIED-S**: the competitor's own page states it, read through a WebSearch extract on 2026-10-21.
  - **REPORTED**: a third party says it. Several of these are rival products, and the cell says so.
  - **unknown (no public source)**: nothing public was found.
  - **inference**: my reading. It is never a fact.
- **Method.** Live pages could not be opened from this container:
  - WebFetch to tsenta.com, ycombinator.com and stork.ai failed with `ENOTFOUND`.
  - curl through the proxy to tsenta.com and simplify.jobs failed with `CONNECT 403`.

  Every competitor fact comes from search extracts. A search model writes those extracts, so wording in quotation marks
  may be paraphrased. Facts carried over from `docs/competitors/tsenta.md` and `simplify.md` (2026-10-19) are marked
  "(2026-10-19)" where this pass did not re-find them.

## Where we lead
1. **Batch approval of exact answers, then agent sends on an exact match, with a kill switch.** Neither rival publishes this. Tsenta offers per-application review or none; Simplify's Autopilot has no review step. Built, but off.
2. **Ask once, before the session, across a whole queue.** Tsenta pauses one application at a time. Simplify's Autopilot fails, and its asking is "not yet live".
3. **Proof per application:** pictures before and after the click, the hash of the approved payload, and an outcome that never says "not sent" after a click. Neither rival publishes screenshots.
4. **Fact provenance**, with a quarantine, an allowlist and a per-answer ledger. No public equivalent at either rival.

## Where we trail
1. **Volume and autonomy today.** 0 real submissions, and the agent mode is off. Tsenta submits from its cloud with the tab closed and claims hundreds a week. Our runs need the person's Chrome open, with the application tab in front.
2. **Coverage.** We cover 4 ATS families. Tsenta claims 19-30+; Simplify claims 100+, including iCIMS, Taleo and SmartRecruiters.
3. **Workday, account creation, tailoring, inbox.** Tsenta creates Workday accounts and tailors with a diff. Our Workday stays card by card, behind a person-made sign-in. Our tailoring is not wired into the extension. Mail confirmation is off.

## What to ship next
1. **The first real agent-submitted application on Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby.** This needs the owner's word to flip `agentSubmits` and `CAREER_AGENT_SUBMIT`. A confirmation email is the proof.
2. **Wire `career_tailor` into the card and the batch.** The tailored file's hash goes into the approval. This closes the most visible feature gap with both rivals.
3. **The next ATS by posting share** (iCIMS or SmartRecruiters), read-and-fill first, then receipts. In parallel, mail confirmation on (`CAREER_MAIL_TRACKING`: the owner's call). Inference: coverage is where the rivals' claims are widest.
