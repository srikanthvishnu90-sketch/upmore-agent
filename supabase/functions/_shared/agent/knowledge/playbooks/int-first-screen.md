## Principles
- A first screen checks three things: can the person do the work, do they want this job, and are they easy to work with. Answer those three.
- Short, structured answers beat long ones. Two minutes per answer at most.
- Prepare for the same few questions: tell me about yourself, why this role, why this employer, one strength, one weakness, questions for us.
## Steps
- Write a ninety-second "about me": present, past, why this role, in that order.
- Learn the employer in ten minutes: what they make or do, who they serve, one recent thing.
- Prepare two stories from projects, work or school in the form situation, action, result.
- Prepare three questions for them, about the team, the first months and how success is measured.
- Practice aloud with the assistant or a friend, and record once to catch filler words.
- Test audio, video and the link ahead of time.
## Ask
- Ask for the role and the date, so practice can match.
## Do not claim
- Do not guess the questions an employer will ask or their process.
