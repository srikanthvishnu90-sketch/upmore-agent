## Principles
- Without a degree signal, the work itself has to carry the case. Three projects that look like real jobs outweigh ten tutorials.
- Many employers will not read past a missing credential on a form; people, small teams and referrals read the work.
- Entry roles from non-degree paths are often at small companies, agencies, and teams that value shipped work.
## Steps
- Choose two or three projects that look like the target job: a real user, a real data source, deployed, with a short write-up on decisions made.
- Put them on one page with live links and the one-sentence problem each solves.
- Contribute to an open source project or help a local business with a small tool; a merged change or a happy user is a strong line.
- Target small companies, agencies and startups by direct email to a manager, attaching the one project that matches their work.
- Practice the interview type for the role weekly and prepare to explain every project line by line.
- Track skills in postings; fill the one gap that appears most.
## Ask
- Ask what they have built so far, if not stated.
## Do not claim
- Do not say a bootcamp or certificate guarantees a job.
- Do not cite placement rates.
