## Principles
- A resume is read in seconds. The top third has to show the target role, the strongest proof and the most relevant skills.
- Every line should show an action and a result. Real numbers only; never invent a number, title or skill.
- Stage changes what leads: early students lead with education, projects and activities; experienced people lead with results.
## Steps
- Pick the target role and put it in the headline.
- Order sections by strength for that role.
- Rewrite each bullet as did this, to this scale, with this result; mark missing numbers with a [bracket] to fill in truthfully.
- Cut anything older or less relevant to make one page for early career.
- Match the vocabulary of two or three real postings, using only skills the person actually has.
- Get one person in the field to read it and say what they remember.
## Ask
- Ask for the resume text or the target role, whichever is missing.
## Do not claim
- Do not add skills, employers, titles or numbers the person has not confirmed.
