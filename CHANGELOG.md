# Changelog

All notable changes to Upmore. Versions follow the release verdicts in evals/results; a version is cut only when `node evals/runner.cjs` says RELEASE OK and the evidence ledger carries the run.

## [Unreleased]

- Control plane: standing instructions, autonomy envelopes with weekly usage, verbatim corrections applied to later requests, undo and mitigation contracts, the kill switch, export without secrets, the deletion plan, the onboarding interview.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-10

- Ledger and analytics over real rows: balances with source and as-of, net worth, safe to spend, 30/60/90 cash flow, budgets with fixed-bill-aware pacing, cards and debts (47 capabilities).
- News relevance: a labeled week of 500 stories across five personas; four scorer defects fixed; alerts decided by freshness and credibility.
- Proactive feed: engine findings become loop inputs with reasons and real deadlines; the weekly money minute.
- Loop approval fixes: confirmations expire, the envelope is checked against the values written, a money confirmation restates amount and recipient, a completed key answers already done.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-10

- Save engine, earn engine, investing without recommending, news brain, money movement logic, compliance map, connector model and catalog.
- Eval harness with release gates and planted mutations; datasets with privacy lint; parity matrix with a coverage ratchet.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-09

- Agent operating model, capability registry with honest status, voice composer with the anti-pattern linter, recovery engine.
