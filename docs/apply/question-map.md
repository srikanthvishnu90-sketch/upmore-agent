## Against independent hand labels (240 random required questions)

- Precision: 97.4% (of 229 questions the map placed). Recall: 94.5%.
- Fill precision: 99.5% of 187 questions it would fill from the person's facts with no one looking first (fact, derived, file). Every other kind is shown in the approval.
- In-sample: the map's rules were written after reading the unknown questions of both captured samples, which the labeled sample is drawn from. A held-out capture is the next measure.
