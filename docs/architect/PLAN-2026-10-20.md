# The plan from here (architect, 2026-10-20)

answers sent; then networking that gets replies. Every task below is ordered by how directly it moves that number.

Owner-only steps are marked **[owner]**. Everything else, the architect and builders do.

Measures that count:
- confirmed applications;
- seconds of the person's attention per confirmed application;
- wrong answers sent (must be 0);
- replies to outreach;
- interviews.

## The efficiency edge (why this beats Simplify and Tsenta)

Everyone fills forms. What limits volume is the person's seconds per application and the questions only they can
answer.

| | Simplify | Tsenta |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Fills one tab while the person drives each posting | Fills unattended |
| What breaks | The person's time | Its reviews complain about wrong answers |

The edge is to do both:
- Prepare the next posting while the person reviews the current one.
- Draft the unique questions from confirmed facts (B1).
- One card per application showing only what is new.
- Every answer given once covers every wording of that question across employers.
- The employer's confirmation captured for each.

Target: 30 seconds of attention per confirmed application (WO-042's budget), with 0 wrong answers sent.
