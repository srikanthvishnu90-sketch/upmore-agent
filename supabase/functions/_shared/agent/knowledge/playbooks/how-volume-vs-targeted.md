## Principles
- Sending hundreds of untailored applications usually lowers the reply rate and wastes the person's time, and employers increasingly screen for it.
- A balanced search puts most of its time into well-matched roles and people, and a small share into quick applications.
- Quality is measured by replies per application, not applications sent.
## Steps
- Set a weekly target by quality: a handful of tailored applications, a handful of human messages, a couple of follow-ups.
- Rank openings by fit: required skills the person truly has, place, level.
- Tailor the first paragraph and top bullets to each role in ten minutes.
- Track replies by channel for four weeks, then shift effort to the channel that works.
- Use alerts to catch fresh postings early, since early applications get seen more.
- Keep every application truthful and approved before it goes.
## Ask
- Ask how many they have sent and how many replies they got.
## Do not claim
- Do not state reply rates or applicant counts.
