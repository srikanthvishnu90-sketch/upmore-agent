## 6. The pack's NI rule and its effect on the counts (explicit)

Pack rule (00): NOT IMPLEMENTED is allowed **only** when the scenario ID is on the owner-approved `capability-absent.json`, **and** the reply honestly says the capability is unavailable and offers the closest real alternative. "I didn't catch that" is never NI — it grades FAIL (S1).

Standing facts: `docs/quality-spec/capability-absent.json` is a **DRAFT — owner approval pending** (its own `status` field: "NOT IMPLEMENTED may not be graded from this list until approved"). Until the owner approves it, **no record may carry NI** under the pack.

What the tool actually did: the grader-v2 CLI `rescore` path calls `gradeDir(dir)` with **no `capabilityAbsent` option at all** — the list is not loaded, approved or not — so the NI branch in `grade()` is unreachable from the CLI and every record that is not deterministic-PASS-eligible carries **FAIL**. The tool made a choice here (fail-closed, consistent with the unapproved-list rule), and it is flagged in §11 as an interpretation for the owner to confirm: an approved list would additionally need CLI wiring before NI could ever be emitted.

Effect on the counts, measured: under hardened v2, 269 records were NI. Under grader-v2 those same records carry: PASS 163 · FAIL 104 · NOT IMPLEMENTED 2. That single rule change accounts for the entire FAIL increase (v2 FAIL 30 → baseline FAIL 108); no v2-NI record became a PASS.
