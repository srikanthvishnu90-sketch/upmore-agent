# Tsenta: what it does, where it is ahead, and where we can be drastically better

Source: tsenta.com landing page text, supplied by the owner on 2026-10-19 (the page as it read that day). Every Tsenta
claim below is Tsenta's own marketing and is UNVERIFIED until checked against users' reviews and live use; the
independent research (reviews, Sonara's shutdown, outcome evidence) is pending and will be added here with sources.

## What Tsenta says it does (its own words, 2026-10-19)
- Watches 50,000+ career pages on 30+ ATSes (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby; "nineteen ATSes covered" elsewhere on
  the same page; "~95% of postings") and submits a tailored resume "the moment a fitting role goes up". "Hundreds of
  applications a week."
- Prep: reads the job description, rewrites bullets, shows an "ATS score", shows every change before sending.
- Apply: login, every field, open-ended questions "in your voice", the upload; a receipt of what was sent.
- Track: connects the person's email, routes recruiter replies to the right application, moves status on its own.
- Channels: web dashboard, iOS and Android apps, iMessage and WhatsApp, a Chrome extension, an MCP server and CLI.
- Price: $19 / 600, $39 / 1,500, $99 / 4,500 applications per 30 days (about $0.02-0.03 each); 25 free; "pay only
  for jobs actually submitted".

A feature-by-feature race against a funded team that is ahead loses. The edges below are structural: things Tsenta's
model (volume, sold by the application) makes hard for it to copy.

## Where Tsenta's own page shows the weakness
- **Its tailoring example invents results.** The original bullet "Built features for the web app using React." becomes
  "Shipped distributed React + TS surfaces serving 4M MAU; cut p95 render time 38%." Neither number is in the bullet
  shown; where they came from is not shown. An invented metric is a claim the candidate must defend in an interview
  and a background check.
- **Its "in your voice" answer is generic praise.** "Why Stripe?" gets "Stripe is the rare company where every layer
  reflects the same obsession with craft", labelled "drafted from your résumé · not generic". It contains nothing from
  a résumé.
- **It sells applications, not outcomes.** Price scales with volume (4,500 a month on the top tier), so its incentive
  is more submissions. Its own demo board shows 47 applied, 8 replied, 4 interviews.
- **"Submitted" is not defined as proven.** The page says "pay only for jobs actually submitted" and shows a receipt
  of fields sent; it does not say an application counts only when the employer confirms it.
- **"ATS score 89 / 100"** is presented as a property of the resume; whether Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby score resumes
  by keywords by default is a research question (pending), not an assumption.

## How we make Trainee drastically better: the efficiency edge is interviews, not applications
Tsenta optimizes applications per dollar. We optimize **interviews per application and per minute of the person's
time**, and we prove every step. Each edge has its measure, so it is a claim we can test, not a slogan.

1. **Truth guarantee with provenance (areas 4, 5).** Every resume line and every answer links to a fact the person
   confirmed; nothing invented, ever; the review shows the source of each line. Measure: invented-claim rate 0 on an
   audited sample of every batch (an automated check against the fact bank, plus a human audit sample).
2. **Proof, not receipts (areas 7h, 9).** "Submitted" only with the employer's confirmation email; UNKNOWN said out
   loud. Measure: share of applications with employer proof; zero "submitted" without proof.
3. **Ask once, by meaning (areas 1, 5).** The person answers about two short questions per application, once, and
   eligibility, legal and consent questions are always theirs. Measure: questions asked per application over time
   (today 5/99 and 31/100 postings need nothing; WO-020 targets both samples).
4. **Fit before volume, and the name protected (areas 3, 7).** A relevance floor and per-employer limits so the person's
   name is never the spammer's. Measure: interview rate per application against the volume baseline. This is a bet:
   if the data shows volume wins for some people (laid off, visa clock), the cap becomes their choice, stated plainly.
5. **The warm path on the best matches (area 11).** For the top few roles, pair the application with a sourced,
   approval-gated note to a relevant person. Tsenta does no networking. Measure: reply rate on outreach, interview rate
   on referred vs cold applications.
6. **After the application (areas 12, 13).** When an interview email arrives, a prep plan built from the exact resume
   version that was submitted and the posting, then offer comparison and negotiation. Tsenta stops at tracking.
   Measure: time from interview email to first practice; offer outcomes.
7. **The conversation is the product (area 14).** Memory and judgment good enough that texting Trainee feels like the
   best assistant the person has used. Measure: the multi-turn conversation eval in the gate (WO-019, 17 of 20).
8. **Cost per outcome (area 15).** Work in the person's own browser and company-portal forms, server browsers only
   where needed; the cost per interview, not per application, is the number we publish to ourselves.

## Parity we still need (no edge survives without the loop working)
One real application proven (P0); Workday and the account-gated portals (D-013, owner decision); freshness closer to
real time; inbox routing; a second channel (web or extension) beside iMessage.
