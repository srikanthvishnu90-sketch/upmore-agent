# Upmore Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 26, 2026**

## The short version

We collect the minimum needed to run your account and your money plan: your name, email, onboarding answers, plan progress, things you save, and your Guide chats. We never sell your data. There are no ads and no third-party trackers in the app. Your chat messages are processed by our AI provider to answer you. You can ask us to export or delete everything at any time.

## What we collect

**Account info.** Your name and email address, from Google sign-in or email + password signup. Passwords are hashed by our auth provider — we never see or store them.

**Onboarding answers.** Your name, US state, hours per week you can put in, paycheck status, and cash you can park. This is what builds your plan.

**Your activity in the app.**
- Plan progress (which steps you've finished on each route)
- Things you save: subscriptions, receipts, ledger entries, renewals, and claims you enter yourself
- Guide chat messages (your questions and the answers)
- Payout proof you submit
- Reminders you set and notification preferences

**What we don't collect.** No payment details, no bank logins, no location tracking, no contacts, no advertising IDs. The optional bank connection (SimpleFIN) is read-only — we see account balances and transactions, we never move money, and we never see your bank password.

## Who processes your data

- **Supabase** — sign-in and database (hosted in the US). Your rows are isolated so only you can see them.
- **Vercel** — hosts the app itself.
- **Anthropic** — your Guide chat messages are sent to Claude so it can answer you. Chats are not used to train models.
- **Google** — only if you choose "Continue with Google" for sign-in.

We don't share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

## Cookies and tracking

The app keeps your sign-in session in your browser's local storage (standard practice, so you stay signed in). There are no third-party analytics, ad trackers, or cross-site cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Ask us to delete your account and we delete your profile, chats, progress, and saved items.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Email the address below and we'll handle it.

## Children

Upmore isn't for kids under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Security

Data moves over encrypted connections (TLS). Database access is restricted per-user. No system is perfect — if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll note it in the app and update the date above.

## Contact

Questions about your data: **support@upmore.app** (provisional — final support address to be confirmed).
