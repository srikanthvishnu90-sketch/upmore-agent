## What 'fixed' looks like (acceptance for the product lane)

1. A no-route turn ends with exactly one clarifying question that names the scenario's own missing input (the job, company, person, posting, or artifact), and offers no discovery output unless discovery was asked.

3. Fallback-axis pass rate, re-measured with this re-scorer over a fresh run, is reported with its denominator — target 100% of no-route turns.
