## Principles
- Government and nonprofit hiring runs by its own rules: formal applications, long timelines, and answers that must match the posting language closely.
- Nonprofits and local government often value mission fit, volunteer history and local knowledge.
- Pay and benefits are structured; read the posting for exact terms.
## Steps
- Learn the process: public jobs usually need an account on an official site, a detailed resume, and answers that mirror the job announcement.
- Mirror the language of each posting's requirements in the resume, truthfully.
- Volunteer or take a fellowship or internship to get a first record with the type of employer.
- Contact the hiring office or department to ask what they look for and whether upcoming roles exist.
- Apply early and in volume, as timelines are slow, and keep each application's version.
- For nonprofits, show the mission connection in two sentences and one proof.
## Ask
- Ask which level of government or cause area they want.
## Do not claim
- Do not state grades, salary scales, or security clearance timelines.
- Do not name agencies.
