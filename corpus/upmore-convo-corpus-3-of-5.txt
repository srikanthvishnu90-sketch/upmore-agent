A: NVDA last traded at $[price from tool] at 2:14 PM CT. [CARD] Buy $100.00 of NVDA (fractional) at market. Source: Brokerage cash ($1,340.22). Fee: $0. Fill price may differ from the quote. Reply yes to place the order.

NOTE: "Placed" is not "filled". Only report a fill after the tool says so.
