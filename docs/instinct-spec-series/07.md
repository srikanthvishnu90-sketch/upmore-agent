Upmore Spec 07: The Save Engine"We need to move into the depths of how you can save money." This doc is that depth: every way the agent finds money the user is losing, ranked by how reliably it converts to real dollars. Each play follows one loop: detect, quantify, act (with the right autonomy tier), verify, book the savings.GoalBuild src/save/: detectors that watch the user's data for loss, and actions that stop it. Every play states expected dollars and the confidence in that estimate. The engine's output is a ranked queue, not a firehose: the proactivity budget (doc 01) decides what surfaces.The playsP1 Subscriptions (the highest-converting play)Detect: BILL-001 recurring detection across all accounts and cards; BILL-002 price increases; BILL-010 free trials ending; BILL-012 duplicates (two music services, overlapping storage plans); BILL-013 unused (no usage signal: no logins in email receipts, no streaming hours visible in card patterns).Quantify: monthly and annual cost, cost since first charge, last usage date.Act: cancel (BILL-003, T3): agent executes via provider API where one exists, browser flow where not, or drafts the exact cancellation email/chat script for the user. Pause or downgrade as the middle option. Annual subscriptions get a pre-renewal surface 14 days out (BILL-011).Verify: BILL-017 cancellation confirmation, then a watch for BILL-018 reactivation charges.Book: savings ledger entry with expected monthly delta.P2 Bill negotiationDetect: bills at or above market rate (internet, mobile, insurance, gym), promotional rates expiring, loyalty tenure.Quantify: market rate from current public plan pricing; typical negotiation outcomes (10-30 percent on telecom).Act: BILL-005. The agent either runs the negotiation chat itself where the provider's chat is accessible (T3 with the user approving the script), or hands the user a word-for-word script with the retention-department path and the specific competing offer to cite.Verify: next bill amount. Book the delta.P3 Fee recoveryDetect: TXN-018 flags overdraft, ATM, wire, foreign transaction, late, and annual fees across every account.Quantify: per fee and annualized.Act: SAVE-005 refund requests (T3): banks refund first-time fees on a polite call more often than not; the agent drafts the request or runs the bank's chat. Prevention where refunds fail: SAVE-006 overdraft prevention moves (T4), account settings changes (decline overdraft coverage on debit).Verify: refund posting. Book it.P4 Rate optimizationDetect: idle cash in near-zero accounts while the user's high-yield options pay 20x more; card balances accruing interest while cash sits idle; CD ladders maturing into default rates.Quantify: exact dollar delta per month of moving X to Y, net of transfer friction.Act: SAVE-002 routing suggestion (T0 information; T3 for the transfer itself via PAY-009). Balance transfer opportunities (SAVE-016, T0 surface, T3 execution).Verify: the transfer posts, the new rate applies.Note: this is the highest-dollar play for most users and the least annoying to surface because it is arithmetic, not nagging.P5 Purchase-time savingsCard selection: CARD-002 tells the user which of their cards earns most for the purchase in front of them (5 percent rotating category vs 2 percent flat).Portal routing: SAVE-012 routes planned online purchases through the user's cash-back portals (Rakuten-style) and card-linked offers (SAVE-013, EARN-010 activation).Price protection and rebooks: SAVE-010 card price-protection claims (GATED), SAVE-011 hotel or flight rebooks when prices drop after booking.This play is advisory by design (T0): the user is standing at a checkout, so the message must be one line and instant.P6 Insurance rebidDetect: INS-003 renewal approaching, premium increases, known cheaper market rates for the user's profile.Act: SAVE-008 shopping (T3): collect quotes through comparison surfaces, present the top 3 with coverage-equivalence notes (a cheaper policy with worse coverage is not savings; say so).Verify: new policy bound, old policy cancelled, no lapse. Book the delta.P7 Debt interest optimizationDetect: CRDT-008 inventory with APRs; SAVE-015 avalanche ordering; SAVE-016 balance transfer math including the transfer fee.Quantify: interest saved per month under each ordering; the payoff date delta from extra payments (CRDT-010).Act: T0 plans and T3 execution. Never shames; the framing is math.P8 Recurring structural savingsAutopay and paperless discounts (CRDT-013), annual-pay discounts on subscriptions, family plan consolidation (SOC-006), employee and affinity discounts the user qualifies for, generic substitution patterns in spending (SAVE-014), utility plan optimization (SAVE-009).The savings ledgerEvery booked play writes to a ledger: play, action taken, expected monthly delta, verified actual delta after one cycle, date. The ledger powers the single most motivating message the agent can send: "you're saving $214/month more than in March, verified." Unverified estimates never mix with verified deltas.Build packet for Claude CodeContext: src/save/ with one detector module per play (detect, quantify, act, verify as the interface), the savings ledger, and the ranking queue. Detectors consume the normalized transaction and bill data from doc 04. Actions reuse doc 06's money workflows and doc 01's confirmation UX.Constraints: no detector fires a user message directly; everything goes through the proactivity budget. Estimates are labeled estimates. Cancellation actions are T3 minimum.Done when: pnpm vitest run src/save passes with a synthetic household fixture (12 subscriptions, 3 negotiable bills, 4 fee events, idle cash, two APR debts): every detector finds its target with no false positives on the fixture's control transactions, the ledger books only verified deltas, and the queue ranks by verified dollars. pnpm typecheck passes.How Claude Code proves it is betterBenchmark against the category leaders: Rocket Money and Monarch's subscription finders. The eval: 30 synthetic financial lives with planted savings plays of known value, plus 10 real-anonymized transaction histories with hand-labeled savings opportunities. Metrics: precision and recall of detection, dollar accuracy of quantification, and end-to-end conversion (did the action actually execute and verify). Upmore wins when recall beats both baselines on the synthetic set (it should: it sees every account, not one) and conversion is provable through the verify step the apps do not do.Previous: Money MovementNext: Earn EngineDraft specification.
