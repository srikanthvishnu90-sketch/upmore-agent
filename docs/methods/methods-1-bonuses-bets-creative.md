# Upmore Methods 1: Bonuses, Bets and Creative Plays

October 11, 2026

## How to read each method card

Each method is one card Upmore can build as a feature. Within a section, methods run from the most money for the time spent to the least. Methods 2 covers money owed, idle cash, investing and side income.

- **What it is:** the method in one sentence.
- **Real example:** a named, current offer with real numbers and the month it was checked.
- **Payout, effort, odds, how often:** what it pays, how long it takes, what can go wrong, and how often it repeats.
- **How Upmore runs it:** how the agent spots it, what it asks the user to approve, and how it tracks it to payout.
- **What Upmore needs:** the data, partner or licence required.
- **Tag:** [Read] looks and reports, [Ask] acts after one approval, [Rule] repeats an approved action, [Flag] is regulated.

Offers change weekly. Every number was checked in October 2026, and Upmore must re-check the live terms before showing one to a user.

## Bank and brokerage bonuses

The best money per hour comes from moving money you already have, with checking bonuses paying less per hour but needing no savings.

### IRA and 401(k) transfer match (creative)

**What it is:** A broker adds a percent of every dollar you move into its IRA, including old 401(k) rollovers.

**Real example:** Robinhood pays non-Gold customers 1% on IRA transfers and 401(k) rollovers all year, with no cap (checked October 2026). Its 2% promo ended October 9, 2026.

- **Payout:** $100 to $1,000 per $10,000 to $100,000 moved, one time.
- **Effort:** 45 to 60 minutes, one time, plus waiting 1 to 2 weeks.
- **Odds and risk:** Payout is near certain once the transfer settles. Moving out within 5 years triggers a prorated early removal fee.
- **How often:** Per transfer, while the offer runs.

**How Upmore runs it:**

1. Spots an IRA or old 401(k) from statement emails or linked balances, and notes the balance and current broker.
2. Shows the dollar match, the 5-year hold, and any fees at the old broker. Asks the user to approve starting the move.
3. Prefills the transfer form, then watches email and balances for settlement and the match deposit. Sets a 5-year "do not move" reminder.

**What Upmore needs:** Account aggregation, email read access, an offer database, and a broker partner or guided handoff.

**Tag:** [Ask] [Flag]

**Source:** [Robinhood Gold IRA transfer bonus](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-gold-match-2026)

**What it is:** A broker pays a percent of assets you move in, spread out as small monthly payments.

**Real example:** Public pays 1% of transferred assets with no cap, started September 14, 2026. It pays in up to 60 monthly installments and covers up to $100 of transfer fees on $5,000 or more (checked October 2026).

**What Upmore needs:** Account aggregation, email read access, offer terms feed, and a broker handoff link.

**Tag:** [Ask] [Flag]

## Credit card bonuses and rewards plays

Welcome bonuses pay the most per hour, and the lesser-known plays add steady money from cards the user already holds.

**What it is:** A new card pays a big points bonus after you reach a spending target in the first months.

**Real example:** Chase Sapphire Preferred pays 75,000 points after $5,000 of purchases in 3 months, with a $95 annual fee (checked October 2026).

- **Effort:** 15 minutes to apply, then normal spending for 3 months.
- **Odds and risk:** Approval is likely under 5 of 24. Missing the spend target, or carrying a balance, wipes out the gain.
- **How often:** Per card, subject to each issuer's repeat rules.

**How Upmore runs it:**

1. Reads monthly card spend to confirm the user can reach $5,000 in 3 months without extra buying.

3. Tracks spend toward the target with a deadline, then confirms the points post within 6 to 8 weeks.

**What Upmore needs:** Transaction data, email read access, card affiliate partner, and an application rules ledger.

**Source:** [Chase Sapphire Preferred](https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred)

**Real example:** Spectrum Contract Buyout covers an early termination fee up to $500. You must order internet plus TV or voice. Payment comes within 10 business days after approval. Checked October 2026.

- **Payout:** Up to $500, once.
- **Effort:** About 1 hour.
- **Odds and risk:** Only covers the cancel fee, not taxes or equipment fees. The required bundle may cost more each month.

3. Submits the final bill showing the fee and tracks the payment.

**What Upmore needs:** Email and bank data, address-level provider lookups.

### Claim automatic airline refunds

**What it is:** Airlines owe cash back for canceled flights, big schedule changes, and very late bags.

**Real example:** A change over 3 hours domestic or 6 hours international earns a refund if you decline the new flight. A bag over 12 hours late refunds the bag fee. Reported by TravelPulse. Checked October 2026.

- **Odds and risk:** Accepting a voucher or new flight can waive the refund. Refunds should arrive in 7 business days on a card.
