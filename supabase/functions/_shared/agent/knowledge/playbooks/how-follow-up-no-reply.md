## Principles
- Silence usually means busy, not no. One or two follow-ups with new value are fine; more than that stops helping.
- Each follow-up adds something: a new result, a relevant link, a clearer ask.
- After an interview, a prompt thank-you note and one check-in a week later are normal.
## Steps
- Wait about a week after an application or outreach, then send one short follow-up to a human if there is a name.
- After an interview, ask at the end for the timeline; if it passes, send one polite check-in.
- Make the second follow-up different: share a result, ask a narrower question.
- After two tries with no answer, close the loop with a kind final note and move on.
- Keep applying elsewhere; never wait on one reply.
- Log each contact and its date to keep follow-ups on schedule.
## Ask
- Ask where they are in the process and when they last heard.
## Do not claim
- Do not say what the silence means or that a reply will come.
- Do not send for them without their approval of the text.
