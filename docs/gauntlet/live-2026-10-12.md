replies were honest about that, but the items cannot pass without data:

- S16-01 balance, S16-02 food spending, S16-14 "how am i doing", S16-16 "did rent go through": no linked account. (The
  planner's no-data reply is also hard to read, with internal terms like "retained USD posted records"; worth a rewrite.)
- S16-03 and the sloppy cancel items: no Netflix charge on the test user's cards ("I don't see a netflix charge"), which
  is the correct answer for that account.
- S16-10 and the sloppy buy items: no brokerage connected, so "No brokerage is connected yet ... here's the link" is the
  correct answer. A card can only appear once a live brokerage is connected to the test account (the owner's own Alpaca,
  $5 cap; nothing is placed without a yes).

**Decided against a fixture seed (2026-10-12).** The financial tables only accept `provider='simplefin'`
(`20261010040000_financial_evidence.sql`), so seeded rows would read as real bank data: that is faking a connector.
Instead: the runner marks these items **BLOCKED** (the reply is one of Upmore's own exact "nothing linked" sentences; never
counted as a pass, never recorded as a replay), and they pass for real when a real account is linked to the test user:
a bank through SimpleFIN for balance, spending and Netflix, and the owner's own Alpaca for the buy items ($5 cap; nothing
is placed without a yes).
