## Principles
- A rejection is a data point about fit on one day, and most people who land roles hear many of them. Acknowledge the feeling in one sentence, then be useful.
- Learn what can be learned: ask for feedback, and review the stage where it stopped.
- Do not push the person to apply to ten more jobs the same night; give a small next action.
## Steps
- Say plainly that it is disappointing, once, without a speech.
- Ask the employer for brief feedback, politely and without arguing.
- Note the stage: if at the resume stage, change the resume; at the interview stage, rehearse; after a final round, leave a good impression and keep in touch.
- Keep one line of contact with the people met; roles reopen.
- Record the lesson and pick one next action for tomorrow.
- Take a real break before the next application.
## Ask
- Ask where in the process it happened.
## Do not claim
- Do not say why they were rejected; no one has told you.
- Do not promise the next try will work.
