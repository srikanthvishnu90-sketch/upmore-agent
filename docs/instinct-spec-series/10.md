Upmore Spec 10: Financial News and Intelligence"How can we inform them of financial news, for example everything in the WSJ, TechCrunch acquisitions, investments that may be interesting, how do we personalize it." This doc is the news brain: what comes in, how it is scored against what the user owns and cares about, and when it is allowed to interrupt.GoalBuild src/news/: ingestion from licensed and public sources, entity resolution against the user's financial life, relevance scoring, personalized delivery formats, and an alert policy that respects the proactivity budget.SourcesThree classes, with honest licensing notes:1Licensed or API-accessible: WSJ and similar paywalled outlets are licensed products. "Everything in the WSJ" is achievable through licensed feeds/APIs where available, or headline-and-summary level through their RSS, with links out for full text. Do not scrape paywalled full text; that is both against terms and unnecessary, since the agent's job is relevance and summary, not republishing.2Public and wire: TechCrunch and general tech/business press (RSS and public APIs), SEC EDGAR filings (8-K, S-1, 13F - acquisitions, IPOs, insider moves), company press releases, Fed and macro calendars, earnings calendars.3Market data: quotes, prices, and fundamentals from a market data provider (this powers the numbers in alerts; news text never carries unverified prices).The pipeline1Ingest: poll RSS/APIs on a schedule (15 minutes for general, 5 for breaking wires), subscribe to EDGAR feeds for watched tickers.2Dedup and cluster: the same acquisition announced in 40 outlets is one story, one alert. Cluster by entities and event, keep the best source.3Entity resolution: link every story to tickers, private companies, sectors, and people. This table is the heart of personalization: story -> entities -> user's holdings, watchlist, screens, employer, past reads.4Score: relevance = holdings match (owned names outrank everything) + watchlist match + sector/theme overlap with the user's screens + acquisition or funding events in spaces the user reads + freshness + source authority. Pure market noise (daily "stocks mixed" pieces) scores near zero by design.5Decide: per doc 01's decision policy: breaking on an owned name can interrupt (T1 alert); everything else waits for the brief or a request.Delivery formatsMorning brief (NEWS-004, opt-in): 5-8 items max, ordered by personal relevance, each with a two-sentence summary and a link. First item is always the most portfolio-relevant, not the most popular.Breaking alert (NEWS-005): owned name + material event (acquisition, earnings surprise, guidance cut, regulator action, executive departure). One message: what happened, what it touches in the user's money, link. No interpretation framed as advice; the advice guard (doc 09) applies here too.Acquisitions tracker (NEWS-006): TechCrunch and EDGAR-driven deal flow with a standing view: this week's deals, sizes, acquirers, and any overlap with the user's private holdings or watchlist.Deep dive on request (NEWS-009): multi-source synthesis on a company or theme, with citations, when the user asks.Weekly sector roundup (NEWS-013): the user picks sectors; one message, skimmable.Personalization loopSignals: which brief items the user taps, expands, asks follow-ups on, or dismisses; explicit "more like this" / "less of this" / "mute this topic" controls available on every item via reply.Learning: per-user topic and entity weights updated from signals; decay over time so interests can change; everything inspectable ("why am I seeing this" is answerable from the weights).Cold start: holdings and watchlist drive early relevance before any behavior exists. A user who owns NVDA, PLTR, and Nebius gets AI infrastructure news on day one.Anti-filter-bubble: NEWS-014 audit - the brief must include one item outside the user's usual topics when something is broadly material; the weights can narrow but never blind."Investments that may be interesting"Handled within the doc 09 boundary: the news engine surfaces events (an IPO filing in a sector the user follows, an acquisition of a company on their watchlist, a tender offer on a private holding) as information with facts and links. The decision framing stays with the user, always.Build packet for Claude CodeContext: src/news/ with ingest.ts (feed adapters with a fixture feed for tests), cluster.ts, entities.ts, score.ts, brief.ts, alerts.ts, personalize.ts. Consumes holdings from doc 04 connectors and the watchlist from doc 09. Delivery through doc 01's loop and doc 11's composition.Constraints: no paywalled full-text scraping; summaries link out. Alert texts pass the advice guard. Quiet hours enforced. Dedup must collapse syndicated repeats of the same story.Done when: pnpm vitest run src/news passes on a fixture firehose (200 stories with planted duplicates, 3 portfolio-relevant events, 1 acquisition): clustering produces one story per event, the 3 relevant events score above the alert threshold, the brief orders by personal relevance, mute feedback suppresses the topic in the next brief. pnpm typecheck passes.How Claude Code proves it is betterThe eval is precision and recall of relevance, judged against hand-labeled ground truth: build a labeled set of 500 stories (relevant / not relevant) for 5 fixture personas with different holdings and interests. Metrics: brief precision (fraction of brief items rated relevant, target over 80 percent), alert recall (fraction of material owned-name events that alerted, target 100 percent on the labeled set), and alert precision (no more than 1 non-material interruption per week at fixture volume). Baselines: a raw WSJ/TechCrunch feed (firehose, zero precision) and a generic finance app's notification stream. Upmore wins on both metrics because baselines each fail one of them.Previous: Investing BoundaryNext: Response QualityDraft specification. Nothing here is financial advice; nothing moves money.
