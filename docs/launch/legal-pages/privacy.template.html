Trainee Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Trainee is a career agent you talk to by text (SMS or iMessage), Telegram, WhatsApp or the web. It finds jobs, tailors your resume, fills applications with answers you confirmed, sends nothing without your yes to the exact content, and tracks how each application goes. This policy says what Trainee collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how to delete it. "Trainee", "we" and "us" mean {{LEGAL_NAME}}, which runs Trainee.

Google user data. Trainee's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will also adhere to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy. Google user data is never used to create, train or improve a machine learning or artificial intelligence model. Details are in "Your Google account (Gmail)" below.

What we collect

  • Contact and account details: the name and phone number you give us when you sign up, the messaging account you use (for example your Telegram user ID), and your email address if you give it to Trainee.
  • What you tell Trainee: your messages, the jobs you want, and the facts you confirm about yourself (education, work history, work authorization and answers to application questions). Trainee only fills an application with facts you confirmed; it never makes one up.
  • Your resume: the file you send and the text read from it, and the tailored versions Trainee makes for each job.
  • Your applications: the jobs you approved, the exact answers you approved, what each employer's form showed after submitting (the confirmation reference and a short excerpt), and each application's stage.
  • Data from accounts you connect: only if you connect them. For Google, see below.
  • Browser extension: see "The Trainee — Assisted Apply extension".

How we use it

  • To run the features you ask for: finding jobs, tailoring your resume, filling and sending the applications you approve, tracking their progress, and replying to you.
  • To keep Trainee secure and working: preventing abuse, investigating problems you report, and meeting our legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to decide credit or lending.

Who we share it with

  • Employers, when you approve an application: the exact answers and resume you approved go to that employer's application form.
  • Service providers that run Trainee for us, under contracts that limit them to doing so: Supabase (our database and servers), OpenAI (the AI model that reads your messages and writes Trainee's replies; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models), Vercel (our website and, if you use cloud apply, the browser that fills the employer's form), and the messaging service you chose: Telegram, LoopMessage (SMS and iMessage) or Meta (WhatsApp).
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or of Trainee.

Text messaging: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Your Google account (Gmail)

Connecting Google is optional. Trainee works without it: you can type an emailed code yourself and tell Trainee how an application went. If you connect it, Trainee asks Google for these permissions only:

Permission Google showsWhat Trainee does with it
See your primary Google Account email address (openid, email)Shows you which Google account you connected.
View your email messages and settings (gmail.readonly)Two things only. 1. Security codes: while an application you approved is being sent, Trainee looks for the security code the application system (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or Workday) just emailed you, from that system's own sending address and authenticated by Gmail, and types it into that form. 2. Application tracking: Trainee reads the sender, subject, date and Gmail's short preview of each new email (never the full email) to find employers' confirmations, rejections, interview invitations and replies, and updates the stage of that application in your tracker.

Trainee cannot send, delete, label or change any of your email with these permissions. If Trainee later offers sending email from your Gmail, it will ask you for that permission separately, at the moment you want to use it, and will only send an email you approved word for word.

What Trainee keeps from Gmail, and for how long

  • Security codes: never stored. A code is held in memory only until it is typed into the form, and never shown in the chat, written to a log, or kept with the application.
  • Emails it reads for tracking: not stored. For an email from an employer about one of your applications, Trainee keeps only the result (for example "Acme: interview"), the employer, the role, one short evidence line taken from the subject, the email's date, and a one-way fingerprint of Gmail's message ID so the same email is never counted twice. For every other email it keeps nothing. It also keeps the time it last read up to, so it does not read the same mail again, and any follow-up or thank-you draft it wrote for you from a tracked email.
  • Access to Google: the tokens Google issues are stored encrypted in our database's secret store, are used only by Trainee's servers, and never reach the chat, your browser or the AI model.
  • The tracked email results and the drafts are kept until you disconnect Google, say "forget everything", or delete your account. The time it last read up to is kept until you disconnect Google or delete your account: "forget everything" does not delete it, so that Trainee does not read the same mail again and bring back what you just erased.

Who sees it

  • No person at Trainee reads your email or the data Trainee keeps from it, unless you ask us to for a support request and agree to it for specific emails, it is needed for security (for example investigating abuse), or the law requires it.
  • When an employer email is unclear, Trainee may ask OpenAI's model for a second opinion. It sends only the sender's name and domain, the subject and the preview, with long numbers and email addresses masked. OpenAI processes it for Trainee only, and does not use it to train its models.
  • Gmail data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to decide credit or lending, and never transferred to anyone else except as described here, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of Trainee with your consent.

Disconnecting Google

Say "disconnect gmail" to Trainee at any time. Trainee revokes its access at Google, deletes the stored tokens, and deletes the tracked email results, drafts and read time described above. If Google doesn't confirm the revoke, or anything can't be deleted, Trainee tells you so in its reply. If a mail check was already running when you disconnected, it can save what it had read just before; Trainee stops it from saving anything it reads after, and saying "disconnect gmail" again deletes whatever it saved. Application stages already updated in your tracker and messages already in your chat stay as part of your history until you say "forget everything" or delete your account. You can also remove Trainee's access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information while your account is open. Saying "forget everything" to Trainee deletes your profile, confirmed facts, tracker, tailored resumes and mail tracking results. To delete your whole account, email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the phone number you signed up with; deleting it removes your stored data and connected-account tokens, and we confirm when it is done. Copies in our providers' backups are deleted on their own schedules.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Each person can read only their own records, connected-account tokens are kept in an encrypted secret store, and only Trainee's servers can use them. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you.

Your choices

  • See what Trainee knows: ask "what do you know about me?" or "what's connected?".
  • Correct a fact by telling Trainee the right one.
  • Stop texts: reply STOP. Disconnect Google: say "disconnect gmail".
  • Delete: say "forget everything", or ask us to delete your account.

The Trainee — Assisted Apply extension

The browser extension fills Greenhouse job application forms in your own browser from the saved answers in your Trainee account, and records the employer's confirmation after you submit an application yourself.

  • Your sign-in: your email and password go only to Trainee's own sign-in service. Your password is never stored. A sign-in token is kept on your device until you sign out.
  • Your saved answers: your name, contact details, education, work history and confirmed screening answers sync from your Trainee account to your browser over your signed-in session, and are stored on your device while you are signed in. Signing out deletes them from the device.
  • The application page: on Greenhouse, the extension reads the form to fill it and reads each filled field back to check it.
  • Your proof: after you submit, the extension reads the employer's confirmation page (the confirmation reference and a short excerpt around it, the page address, and the answers that were submitted) and sends them to your own Trainee tracker, where they become your application record. A screenshot of the confirmation page is kept on your device only and is never sent anywhere.
  • It never submits an application for you, solves a captcha, or checks a legal attestation. It never sells or shares your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party, and it contains no analytics or tracking. A field with no confirmed answer in your account is left empty for you.

Children

Trainee is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in Trainee before a change that affects how your Google data is used takes effect.
