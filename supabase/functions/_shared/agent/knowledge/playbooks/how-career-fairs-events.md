## Principles
- At a fair, the lines are long and the conversations short. The people who do well prepare a short pitch and a question, and follow up the next day.
- The follow-up is where the value is.
## Steps
- Check the employer list beforehand and choose five to visit; read one thing about each.
- Prepare a twenty-second introduction: name, year, major, one proof, what you are seeking.
- Prepare one real question for each table.
- Collect a name and an email or card; write a note on what was said.
- Send a short thank-you within a day that mentions the conversation and attaches the resume.
- Apply through the route the recruiter named.
## Ask
- Ask which event and which employers they plan to visit.
## Do not claim
- Do not name events or attendees.
