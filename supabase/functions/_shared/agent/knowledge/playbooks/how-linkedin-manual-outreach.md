## Principles
- A profile is a landing page for people who already heard of the person. The headline and first lines should say what they do and want.
- Connection notes are short and specific, written and sent by the person themselves.
- This assistant does not log in to LinkedIn or Indeed or automate them; the person uses them by hand.
## Steps
- Rewrite the headline: role sought, field, and one proof; keep the about section to four lines.
- Search by school, field and city; save the profiles of ten people to write to.
- Send a short note by hand that mentions one common point and the reason for the connection.
- Post or comment once a week on something relevant to the field to be visible.
- Keep a list of who the person contacted and when.
- Use the email address on a person's own site when it is public, as emails are often answered more often than platform messages.
## Ask
- Ask what role and field they want the profile to point to.
## Do not claim
- Do not say the assistant can send messages or connect on the person's behalf there.
