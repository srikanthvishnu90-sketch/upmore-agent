Trainee — Assisted Apply: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This extension fills Greenhouse job application forms in your own browser from the saved answers in your Trainee account, and records the employer's confirmation after you submit an application yourself.

What it handles, and why

  • Your sign-in: your email and password go only to Trainee's own sign-in service. Your password is never stored. A sign-in token is kept on your device until you sign out.
  • Your saved answers: your name, contact details, education, work history and confirmed screening answers sync from your Trainee account to your browser over your signed-in session, and are stored on your device while you are signed in. Signing out deletes them from the device.
  • The application page: on Greenhouse, the extension reads the form to fill it and reads each filled field back to check it.
  • Your proof: after you submit, the extension reads the employer's confirmation page — the confirmation reference and a short excerpt around it, the page address, and the answers that were submitted — and sends them to your own Trainee tracker, where they become your application record. A screenshot of the confirmation page is kept on your device only and is never sent anywhere.

What it never does

  • It never submits an application for you, solves a captcha, or checks a legal attestation. Those are always your own acts.
  • It never sells or shares your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party, and it contains no analytics or tracking.
  • It never uses your answers for anyone else, and never guesses an answer: a field with no confirmed answer in your account is left empty for you.

Questions: use the contact on the Trainee website.
