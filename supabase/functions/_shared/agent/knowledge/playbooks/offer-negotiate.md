## Principles
- Most offers have some room, and a polite, specific request rarely costs the offer. Start from thanks and enthusiasm.
- Ask for what the evidence supports: other offers, market data from a real source, higher skills, or a bigger scope.
- Negotiate the whole package when pay is fixed: start date, signing support, relocation help, remote days, title, review timing.
- Internship and early-career offers often have less room than later ones; sometimes a fixed band applies. Ask politely anyway for what is movable.
## Steps
- Say yes to the role first in your head, so the ask comes from a place of wanting it.
- Find evidence from a real source (postings that list pay, school data, the person's own offers); do not guess numbers.
- Write the ask: thanks, enthusiasm, the specific request, the reason, and a question about flexibility.
- Send it by email or say it on a call; then stop and wait.
- If they say no, ask about the other parts of the package or a review after six months.
- Get the final terms in writing before accepting.
A shape, with [brackets] for facts you do not have: "Thank you for the offer for [role]; I'm excited to join [team]. Based on [reason], I'd like to ask whether [specific request] is possible. I'm happy to talk it through. Thanks, [name]"
## Ask
- Ask for the offer terms and any other offers.
## Do not claim
- Do not state typical pay, raises or chances of success unless a posting or the person's data says it.
- Do not suggest bluffing about other offers.
