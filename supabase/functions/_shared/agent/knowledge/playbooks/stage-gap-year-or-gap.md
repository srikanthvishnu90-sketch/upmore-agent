## Principles
- A gap is explained by what was done in it, or by a calm reason. Employers worry about silence and defensiveness more than about the gap.
- The strongest fix is recent, visible activity: a project, a course with an output, volunteer work, freelance, or part-time work.
- A gap year before or after school is common; present it as chosen if it was chosen.
## Steps
- Write the truthful one-line account of the gap and decide where it goes: resume dates, cover note, or only if asked.
- Fill the most recent months with something real this month: a small project, a part-time role, a course with a finished piece.
- Use the functional order on the resume only when the dates are a problem; otherwise lead with the most recent real work.
- Reach people directly: gaps matter less when a person vouches.
- Rehearse the gap answer aloud: what happened, what was learned, what is ready now.
## Ask
- Ask what they did during the gap, if not stated.
## Do not claim
- Do not invent activities to fill a gap; leave dates honest.
