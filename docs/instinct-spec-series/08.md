Upmore Spec 08: The Earn EngineThe other half of "the depths of how you can save and earn money." Earning here means real, claimable dollars: bonuses, benefits, rewards, refunds, and found money. Same loop as save: detect, quantify, act, verify, book.GoalBuild src/earn/: detectors and actions for every legitimate earnings surface, each with requirements tracking, deadline management, and honest effective-rate math. The earn engine's distinct risk is wasting the user's time for pennies, so every play carries the time cost next to the dollars.The playsE1 Bank account bonuses (the biggest reliable dollars)Detect: current signup bonuses across banks ($200-$3,000 for opening accounts with direct deposit or balance requirements).Track: EARN-002 requirement checklist per bonus: direct deposit amount and deadline, debit transaction counts, minimum balance days. The agent watches the user's actual transactions against the checklist.Act: T0 surface with the real math (effective return, time to payout, early-closure fee terms); T3 for opening applications through partner rails where they exist, handoff otherwise.Alert: the day a requirement is met, the day the bonus posts, and 30 days before any early-closure window ends.E2 Employer benefits (the most underclaimed money)Detect: EARN-005 audit from payroll connector: 401k match percent, ESPP discount, HSA seed money, wellness stipends, commuter benefits, education reimbursement, employee discounts.Act: capture reminders (SAVE-019 match check, EARN-006 ESPP windows), enrollment walkthroughs during open enrollment (INS-009).Quantify: dollars per year being left. "Your ESPP discount is worth about $1,900/year and you're not enrolled" is the highest-value sentence this engine can produce.E3 Found moneyUnclaimed property: EARN-007 searches state unclaimed property databases (public data) for the user's name and past addresses; states hold billions in uncashed checks, old deposits, forgotten accounts.Class actions: EARN-008 matches the user's known purchases and accounts against open settlements; files claims (T3) with deadlines tracked.Recalls: EARN-015 matches purchase history against recalls with refund programs.Flight compensation: EARN-016 files delay/cancellation claims (T3) when itineraries from the email connector hit compensable events.E4 Rewards optimizationEARN-011 values every points balance at real redemption rates, not marketing rates.EARN-012 flags transfer sweet spots and expiring balances.EARN-010 activates card-linked offers before they lapse.The standing rule: points are a currency with an issuer-controlled exchange rate; the agent reports the rate honestly.E5 Referral inventoryEARN-003 lists every referral program across the user's accounts with current payouts; EARN-004 tracks referred signups to payout.Act: T0 inventory; the agent never spams the user's contacts. Sharing a referral link is always user-initiated (T3, reviewed message).E6 Side income surfacesEARN-013: matched to the user's actual skills and time, with the Freecash/Propel-style earn apps included but honestly rated: effective hourly rate computed from requirement structure, cash-out fees included.The agent's distinct value: it tracks milestone requirements across every earn app at once and pings the user only when a payout threshold is crossed.E7 Interest and yield on existing moneyOverlaps P4 in the save engine: idle cash earning nothing is an earn problem too. The engines share the rate-optimization module rather than duplicating it.The earnings ledgerSame discipline as the savings ledger: expected vs verified, never mixed. Earn plays carry an extra column: time cost, so the weekly summary can say "$340 found this month, about 40 minutes of your actions."Build packet for Claude CodeContext: src/earn/ with detector modules per play, requirement trackers as data (checklist + evidence queries against transactions), the earnings ledger, deadline scheduler feeding doc 01's follow-up system. Shares the rate module with src/save/.Constraints: no auto-enrollment in anything (T3 minimum); no contacting other people for referrals without a reviewed message; effective hourly rates shown for every time-costing play; deadlines are hard alerts 7 days out and 1 day out.Done when: pnpm vitest run src/earn passes on the synthetic household fixture extended with: a bank bonus mid-requirements, an unclaimed property hit, an ESPP window, expiring points, and a class action match. Every detector finds its target; the requirement tracker follows the fixture's transactions correctly; deadline alerts fire on schedule.How Claude Code proves it is betterBenchmark: against the earn apps themselves (Propel, Freecash) and against doing nothing. Metrics on the fixture set: dollars detected per surface, requirement-tracking accuracy (did it notice the direct deposit that qualified), deadline misses (must be zero), and false-positive rate on found money (wrong-person matches on unclaimed property must be caught by the name/address verification step before the user ever hears about them). The betterment bar: the engine finds at least one verified dollar surface the baselines miss in 8 of 10 fixture lives, and never sends the user on a false chase.Previous: Save EngineNext: Investing BoundaryDraft specification.
