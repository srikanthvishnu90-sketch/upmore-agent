Evidence:

- **What the filter reads** (`155-career-batch-apply.js` prepareRun, uncommitted Fix 2): `fams = Jobs.askRoleFamilies(run.familyText)` → `["SWE"]` for "software engineering intern"; `wantIntern = Jobs.seniorityBand(run.familyText, "") === "intern"` → true. Per posting it reads `p.role_family` (fallback `Jobs.roleFamily(p.title || p.role, p.jd_text || p.jdText)`) and, when wantIntern, `p.seniority_band` (fallback `Jobs.seniorityBand(...)`); a posting is kept iff family ∈ fams AND band === "intern".
- **Fixture shape** (`tests/career-loop-input-writer.test.cjs`, MARKET): 6 postings carrying NO `role_family`/`seniority_band` fields at all — classification is recomputed from titles. The guard therefore never exercises a posting whose family/band must come from JD text, or whose title lacks either token.
