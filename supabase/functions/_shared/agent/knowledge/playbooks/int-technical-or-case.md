## Principles
- Technical and case interviews test thinking out loud. Interviewers want structure, clear assumptions and recovery from mistakes more than a perfect answer.
- Practice in the real format: timed, spoken, with a blank page.
- Know the basics of the role's toolset cold, and be honest about gaps.
## Steps
- Read the posting and list the five skills it names most; rank by weakness.
- Do a short drill every day on the weakest one, with a timer.
- Practice thinking aloud: restate the problem, state assumptions, outline an approach, then work it.
- Do two mock sessions with another person before the real one.
- Prepare to talk through every project on the resume in depth.
- If stuck in the interview, say what you know and ask a clarifying question.
## Ask
- Ask what role and which kind of test they expect.
## Do not claim
- Do not state what a specific employer's interview contains.
