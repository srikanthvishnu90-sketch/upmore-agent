Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

The short version. We collect the minimum needed to run your account and your money plan: your name, email, onboarding answers, plan progress, things you save, and your Guide chats. We never sell your data. No ads, no third-party trackers. Your chat messages are processed by our AI provider to answer you. You can ask us to export or delete everything at any time.

What we collect. Account info: name and email, from Google sign-in or email + password (passwords are hashed — we never see them). Date of birth: to confirm you're 18 or older. Onboarding answers: name, US state, hours per week, paycheck status, cash you can park. Your activity: plan progress, subscriptions / receipts / ledger entries / renewals / claims you enter, Guide chat messages, payout proof you submit, reminders, notification preferences, and your records of Terms acceptance and connection consents. If you connect your bank (SimpleFIN): a read-only bank-access credential stored encrypted in our database vault so the app can sync your accounts — we never see or store your bank username or password. If you connect your brokerage (Plaid): a read-only brokerage-access credential stored encrypted in our database vault so the app can show your holdings — we never see or store your brokerage username or password. If you use the subscription-cancellation agent: the merchant logins you choose to save, stored encrypted in our database vault and used only to carry out cancellations you approve.

What we don't collect. No payment details, no bank or brokerage logins, no location tracking, no contacts, no ad IDs. The optional bank connection (SimpleFIN) and brokerage connection (Plaid) are read-only — we see account balances, transactions, and holdings, we never move money, and we never see your bank or brokerage passwords.

Who processes your data. Supabase (sign-in + database, US — your rows are isolated to you), Vercel (hosts the app), Anthropic (your Guide messages go to Claude so it can answer; chats aren't used for training), Google (sign-in only, if you choose it), SimpleFIN (only if you connect your bank — your bank sign-in happens on SimpleFIN's site; they hand the app a read-only access credential), Plaid (only if you connect your brokerage — your brokerage sign-in happens in Plaid's secure window; they hand the app a read-only access token). Nobody else. We never sell your data.

Cookies & tracking. Your sign-in session lives in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. No third-party analytics or ad trackers.

How long we keep it. While your account is active. Disconnecting your bank deletes your connection record and the stored bank credential. Deleting your account deletes your profile, chats, progress, saved items, your bank connection including the stored bank credential, and any merchant logins you saved for the cancellation agent.

Your rights. Ask us any time to see, correct, export, or delete your data: vishnusrikanth8@gmail.com.

Age. Upmore is for adults 18 and older.

Security. Everything moves over encrypted connections; database access is restricted per user. If we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you.

Terms of Service. The full Terms of Service (v1.0) are at /terms — please read them. The short version: Upmore is a guide, not a guarantee — payouts come from third-party companies with their own terms.
