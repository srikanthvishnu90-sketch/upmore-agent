Upmore Spec 03: Parity With the 10 Largest Non-Investment Finance AppsThe user's bar: "take the 10 most common finance apps, that are not investment platforms - we should have the capability to do all of the things they do." This doc defines that bar app by app, then defines what the agent does that no app can.GoalProduce a parity matrix where every user-facing capability of each of the 10 apps maps to a registry ID (doc 02), and the agent can perform every one through conversation instead of taps. Parity means the user never has to open the app to get the outcome the app exists for.The 10 apps and why they are the setCash App, Venmo, PayPal, Google Wallet, Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Propel, Freecash. They span the four jobs of consumer finance apps: moving money (Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Zelle inside the banks), holding and managing bank money (Chase, BofA, Wells Fargo, Capital One), paying in stores and online (Google Wallet, the bank wallets), and earning rewards and side money (Propel, Freecash, card-linked offers everywhere). Investment platforms (Robinhood, Fidelity) are excluded per the user's instruction; investing is handled on Upmore's own terms in doc 09.App 1: Cash AppWhat it does that Upmore must match: P2P send and request by handle or phone (PAY-001 to PAY-003), Cash Card spending and boost offers (CARD-003, SAVE-013), direct deposit receipt (ALRT-007), instant transfer with fee vs free 1-3 day (PAY-010 with fee disclosure), Bitcoin and stock buying (excluded: investment surface, doc 09 covers user-directed orders only), savings balance with interest (ACCT-018, SAVE-002), tax filing (TAX-012 refund tracking; Upmore does not file taxes itself - doc 14), borrow (small loans - Upmore surfaces cheaper alternatives, CRDT-009, never originates loans itself).Agent edge: the user says "send Marcus $40 for the tickets" in one message instead of seven taps; the agent picks the rail with the lowest fee and says why.App 2: VenmoMatch: P2P with social graph (PAY-001, SOC-001), split purchases (PAY-004), Venmo debit and credit card rewards (CARD-002), crypto (excluded as above), business profiles (out of scope for v1), charity profiles (SOC-008 gifting), purchase protection on eligible payments (dispute drafts, CRDT-equivalent for P2P: PAY-024).Agent edge: no social feed unless the user opts in (SOC-010, default off); the agent remembers who the user actually pays and pre-fills recipients.App 3: PayPalMatch: send and request internationally (PAY-016, GATED), checkout buttons and purchase protection (PAY-024 dispute mitigation), PayPal balance and cash back card (ACCT-001, CARD-002), QR pay in person (deferred: agent cannot hold a phone camera; v1 surfaces the QR code as an image the user shows), bill pay and subscription management (BILL-001, BILL-003), Pay in 4 BNPL (Upmore tracks BNPL plans as debt in CRDT-008 and warns on stacking; it never initiates BNPL - doc 14).Agent edge: cross-rail fee comparison. PayPal's own app never tells the user the check-out rail costs more than the debit card.App 4: Google WalletMatch: tap to pay (NFC - hardware-bound, honestly out of scope; Upmore cannot replace the phone's secure element, doc 14), stored passes and tickets (document vault INS-010 extended), loyalty cards (EARN-011), transaction view across linked cards (TXN-001), P2P via Google (discontinued in the US; noted as a parity trap - do not build dead features).Agent edge: passes and loyalty cards are data, so Upmore can use them: "your Walgreens balance is 8,200 points, use it before the $40 shampoo restock."App 5: ChaseMatch (all national-bank features generalize): Zelle send and request (PAY-012, GATED on bank partnership), bill pay (BILL-008, GATED), mobile check deposit (GATED - requires bank partner camera flow; v1 tells the user to deposit in the bank app and confirms when it posts), account alerts (ALRT family), credit journey score monitoring (CRDT-001), card lock (CARD-011, GATED), dispute a charge (CARD-010), statements (ACCT family, TAX-002 vault), Chase Offers activation (EARN-010), spending summary (ANL-001).Agent edge: Chase's app knows only Chase. Upmore runs the same plays across every bank at once and finds the money between institutions: idle cash in a 0.01 percent Chase savings while a card balance accrues at 24 percent.App 6: Bank of AmericaMatch: everything in the Chase row plus Erica-style virtual assistant Q&A (CORE-002 but actually truthful), Preferred Rewards tier tracking (EARN-011), Bill Pay and eBills ingestion (BILL-007), Keep the Change round-ups (SAVE-001), life events planning content (HOUS-009, HOUS-010).Agent edge: Erica answers inside BofA's walls. Upmore's assistant answers across the whole financial life, including the parts BofA cannot see.App 7: Wells FargoMatch: same national-bank surface: transfers, Zelle, bill pay, card controls, alerts, FICO score (CRDT-001), Fargo assistant Q&A (CORE-002), check deposit (GATED as above), Greenhouse-style budgeting views (ANL family).Agent edge: cross-bank. And honesty: bank assistants are trained to keep money in the bank's products; Upmore has no product to protect.App 8: Capital OneMatch: Eno assistant features: virtual card numbers (CARD-012, GATED), charge explanations (TXN-020), free trial and recurring charge alerts (BILL-010, BILL-001), duplicate charge flags (TXN-009), credit score via CreditWise (CRDT-001), card lock (CARD-011, GATED), Cafe and branch checks (out of scope), shopping offers (EARN-010).Agent edge: Eno is the closest existing product to a piece of Upmore. The betterment harness (doc 13) benchmarks against Eno-style answers directly.App 9: PropelMatch: this is the earn-money app: paid surveys and tasks with cash-out to PayPal or gift cards (EARN-013 side-income surfaces), offer walls with requirement tracking (EARN-002 pattern), referral earnings (EARN-003, EARN-004), cash-out threshold alerts (T1).Agent edge: Propel makes the user hunt for the money. Upmore watches requirements and deadlines and tells the user the day a payout unlocks: "you hit the $25 cash-out on Propel, want it moved to checking?"App 10: FreecashMatch: same earn-app surface: game and app offers with milestone tracking (EARN-002), leaderboard and streak mechanics (gamification - Upmore deliberately does not gamify attention, doc 11; it tracks the money without farming the user), multiple cash-out rails with fee differences (fee comparison at cash-out, T0 advice-free math), promo codes (EARN-014).Agent edge: earn apps bury the effective hourly rate. Upmore computes it: "that offer pays $12 for about 3 hours of play - $4/hour. Your call."The parity matrixBuild evals/parity/matrix.json: rows are the app capabilities above (about 90 after dedup), columns are the 10 apps marking which app has the feature, plus an Upmore column with registry ID and status. The matrix is generated from the registry, never maintained by hand. A capability the apps have that Upmore lacks shows as a red cell with the owning build packet. The two intentional non-parity cells (NFC tap-to-pay, bank check capture) are marked GATED with the exact reason, so the matrix is honest instead of padded.Build packet for Claude CodeContext: evals/parity/matrix.json generator reading the registry, plus per-app checklist files evals/parity/apps/cash-app.md (and nine more) containing the human-readable capability lists from this doc, kept as the audit source.Constraints: the matrix is generated, not handwritten. Non-parity items must carry a reason string. No investment-platform features in the matrix.Done when: pnpm parity regenerates the matrix, prints coverage percent per app, and fails if any app capability lacks either a registry ID or a GATED reason. The CI gate: coverage may never decrease.How Claude Code proves it is betterTwo tests. First, the coverage test above: 100 percent of app capabilities mapped. Second, the conversion test: pick 20 common flows from the apps (send money, split dinner, find a subscription, dispute a charge, deposit check is excluded as GATED), script them as chat conversations, and count messages-to-outcome versus the app's tap count from screen recordings. Upmore wins when every flow completes in conversation with fewer total user actions than the app flow, with the exceptions list matching the GATED set exactly.Previous: Capability InventoryNext: Connector ArchitectureDraft specification.
