## Method

- **Sample:** 39 renders, chosen by surface **type** first, then family —
  every distinct type the career pivot built: connect card (×3 variants),
  connected-accounts list, disconnect (×2), kill-switch state, safety-plan
  card, approval cards for the send / submit / share tiers, list-review
  cards, daily / weekly / quiet-hours digests, season dashboard, company /
  prep / coffee-chat briefs, thesis answers (×2), status answers (×3),
  offer comparison, sensitive-answer confirm, CAPTCHA handoff, ATS render,
  referral end-to-end, refusal, distress, onboarding, resume-diff review,
  autopilot summary, gap-fill brief.
- **Render freshness:** three renders were regenerated in this sweep after
  domain fixes (SC-001 outreach, SC-002 outreach, SC-065 weekly digest) via
  their scenario runners + `scripts/career/shoot.py`; all other renders are
  the canonical evidence as built. No app-template surface exists for the
  career pivot — the conversational renders are the product surface, and
  the harness itself calls the PNG "the chat as the user would see it".
- **Corpus scan (all 406 transcripts, automated, agent prose only):** 194
  renders carry a card — **every one** dumps that card as raw JSON in the
  render (FIN-E-001). "CIR" appears in agent prose in 87 files; snake_case
  tokens in 68; spec-section references (§…) in 28; tier codes (T1/T3) in
  6; cards containing `"fit": null` in 5 files; duplicate company+role
  rows on one card in 2 files. Contrast computed from the render CSS:
  user bubble (white on `#0a84ff`) = 3.65:1; agent bubble (`#111` on
  white) = 18.88:1; card text renders at 12px vs 15px body.

## What passes, honestly

The **prose layer is often genuinely good**: safety-plan, kill-switch,
honest-yield, quiet-hours, onboarding, and status-of-everything renders are
exact, plainly worded, and honest about state — several are models for the
bar. Two swept defects were fixed in this lane (FIN-E-002, FIN-E-003) with
replay tests and regenerated renders. None of that rescues the sweep:
the failures are not scattered typos, they are **systematic** — one
renderer defect hits every card, one status builder leaks an entire other
domain, and internal vocabulary (CIR / §refs / tier codes / snake_case /
raw IDs) recurs across modules and families, including in renders built
after those words were supposedly the house style.

## Verdict

**NO — T-87 does not pass its done-when ("design changes pass the
screenshot sweep") for the career surfaces as built.**

Exactly what fails, in order:

1. **Every card surface fails as rendered** (FIN-E-001, P1): the chat
   render presents each card as a raw JSON `<pre>` block at 12px — 194 of
   406 renders, covering connect, approval (all tiers), safety-plan,
   digest, dashboard, review, handoff, and distress cards. Fix: a
   human-readable card renderer (labeled rows, one primary action,
   consistent anatomy per FIN-E-012), then re-sweep.
2. **The honest-status surface fails** (FIN-E-004, P1): career status
   answers enumerate finance-domain blocked rows (PAY-005 rent, PAY-006
   payment engine) and finance counts/paths. Fix: domain-filter the
   status builder's registry rows to career before rendering.
3. **Internal vocabulary leaks at corpus scale** (FIN-E-006/007, P2):
   CIR (87 files), §refs (28), snake_case prose (68), tier codes incl. a
   card title, UUID/ref IDs and status codes in bubbles. Fix: per-module
   vocabulary pass + a harness lint that fails a render when these tokens
   appear in agent prose.
4. **Approval-card data quality** (FIN-E-005, P2): `fit: null` shown while
   prose claims fit, duplicate roles on one card, un-normalized names.
   Fix at card assembly: dedupe, normalize names, word missing values.

Re-run this sweep after 1–4; the checklist and this sample are the
regression set. The two P2 fixes already landed in this lane (FIN-E-002,
FIN-E-003) show the pattern: domain-module copy fix + replay test +
regenerated render.
