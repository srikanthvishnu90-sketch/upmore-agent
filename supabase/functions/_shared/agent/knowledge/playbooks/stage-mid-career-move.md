## Principles
- Experienced people win on results and references. The resume should show scope, outcomes and trajectory, not duties.
- Most mid-career moves happen through people who already know the work, so the search is mostly conversations.
- A search while employed needs discretion and a clear reason for leaving, stated about the future, not about the past.
## Steps
- Write three achievements with real scope and results that match the target role.
- Tell five trusted contacts the target role and ask for introductions to the people who run those teams.
- Update the profile quietly and set alerts for the exact title and place.
- Prepare the leaving-reason sentence: what the person is moving toward.
- Practice the leadership stories: a decision, a conflict, a failure, a result.
- Ask former managers for references and tell them what to say about.
## Ask
- Ask what is driving the move and what must be better in the next role.
## Do not claim
- Do not state pay bands or promotion timelines.
