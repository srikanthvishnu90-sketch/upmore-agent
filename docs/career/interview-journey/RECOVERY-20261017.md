## Reproduced defects and resulting behavior

The frozen seven-case test reproduced five failures on the published baseline;
the two unknown-before-control cases already passed. The original test and
failed log are retained under `evidence/B5-recovery/`, with their pre-repair
SHA-256 hashes in `freeze.json`.

1. A project-only answer stays eligible after a company correction, while its
   assessment depends on the changed target and becomes stale. Previously, a
   paused `targeted_practice` frame survived without `next_exercise`, and practice
   submission dereferenced undefined. Live and saved checkpoints now require a
   current question-bound assessment and exercise. They return to
   `awaiting_answer` when that support becomes stale, preserving the eligible
   original attempt. Practice submission checks validity before reading the drill.
2. Retrying a failed project assessment after selecting `retry-design` previously
   kept the technical question active. Retry now restores the immutable answer's
   question, rubric and phase. Guided retries restore their supporting assessment,
   exercise and transfer competency, or reject stale support. The saved answer is
   unchanged. Practice, revision and transfer retries are tested across a family
   switch, in addition to the reproduced project case.
3. Pause/stop previously discarded a pending request before its first unknown
   outcome. Every emitted reasoning proposal now retains a references-only
   `checkpoint.outstanding_reasoning` entry. Controls detach the UI checkpoint
   while retaining that action's original digests and binding. Unknown outcomes
   before or after either control survive JSON restart and block another pair.
   New text can still be saved. Trusted terminal settlement removes the fence
   passively, without restoring an old question or requesting a new call.

Outstanding records retain no answer, question, feedback or draft bodies. Existing
v1 states with a pending request or uncertain recovery acquire the record on
their next transition. Records already discarded by an older implementation
cannot be reconstructed by this reducer; A's accounting ledger remains the
authority for outstanding admissions, including after an erase epoch.

Outcome receipts must bind the original action, input and request digests,
original request binding, current validation binding, paired reservation and usage
references. A mismatched receipt cannot release the fence. Terminal settlement
is monotonic: a delayed unknown cannot reopen it. A late semantic candidate cannot
replace the current request. Explicit retries remain separate admissions.
