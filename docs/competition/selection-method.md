# Benchmark selection, version 1

Observed October 10, 2026. Baseline supplied by the owner: Similarweb Android Finance usage table dated September 15, 2026 at [S01](https://www.similarweb.com/top-apps/google/finance/top-free/). The historical September table has not been independently recovered. Its geography remains unverified. The fixed baseline order is Cash App, Google Wallet, Chase Mobile, Venmo, Capital One Mobile, Propel EBT & SNAP Benefits, PayPal, Bank of America Mobile Banking, Freecash: Earn Money, Wells Fargo Mobile.

The current public S01 page differs. It visibly identifies United States, Google Play Store, Finance, Top Free Apps and Usage Rank, and says last updated October 6, 2026. Only five table rows were accessible: Cash App, Google Wallet, Venmo, Chase Mobile and Capital One Mobile. Additional access is offered behind a trial. Its narrative discusses changed ranks while the visible table's change column shows unchanged positions. This conflict is recorded rather than resolved by choosing the narrative. Today's page does not independently prove positions six through ten or the historical September order.

Keep the owner's ten apps and 241 seeds as the required benchmark. Do not silently reorder the baseline or substitute other apps. A later independently verified cohort becomes a new version; it cannot remove requirements from this baseline. PayPal and Venmo remain separate app experiences. Supplemental Monarch, Rocket Money, public/private investing and the earlier 50-scenario/130-capability targets are additional scope, not replacements for this cohort.

The metric is the supplied Android finance-app usage benchmark, not bank size, downloads, ratings, revenue, global financial-business ranking or combined iOS/Android monthly active users. The current page's explicit US selector is evidence about this observation, not retroactive proof of the historical snapshot's geography. No better cross-platform usage ranking has been established in this pass.

Source verification distinguishes owner-supplied seeds, an observed public product claim, documented restrictions, authenticated behavior and personally exercised behavior. Undated product pages record an observation date rather than an invented publication date. Public claims do not establish Upmore API access, permissions or production readiness. Unobserved authenticated behavior stays UNVERIFIED. Competitor NOT FOUND means a scoped search found no support, not that the feature is absent.
