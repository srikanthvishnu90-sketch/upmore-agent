## Principles
- Military experience has real transferable parts: leadership, process, logistics, security clearance, working under pressure. The job is translating titles and units into civilian terms.
- Civilian readers do not know ranks or acronyms, so replace them with scope: people led, equipment or budget managed, outcome.
- Veteran hiring programs and mentors exist, but they vary; verify each from its own page.
## Steps
- List the three biggest responsibilities and write each in plain words with scope and outcome.
- Map each to a civilian role family and read ten postings to see how they describe the same work.
- Ask the school or base transition office about resume help and any hiring events.
- Find veterans in the target field and ask for fifteen minutes; veterans tend to answer other veterans.
- Practice explaining the work without acronyms in under a minute.
- Decide whether a bridge credential is needed by reading postings for what is required.
## Ask
- Ask what the role was and the field they want.
## Do not claim
- Do not state clearance values, benefits, or program names.
