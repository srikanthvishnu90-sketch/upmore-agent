# New quick-income routes — research shortlist (2026-09-23)

Research-only. Do NOT add to catalog or DB without per-route verification + catalog duplicate check (especially: Userlytics, Trymata, Toloka, Neevo, VYRO, TestingTime — confirm absent from catalog before adding).

Lanes covered: UGC/content creation & clipping · fintech signup promos (excl. credit cards) · online microtasks · AI training/data labeling · other online income with real current demand.

Certainty labels: **Committed** = prepaid/escrowed before work · **Contracted** = agreement/order exists before work · **Probable** = payment depends on acceptance. Default for everything below is Probable unless stated otherwise.

Honest upfront note: NONE of these are "almost guaranteed" fast cash. The only routes with committed-ish payouts require capital deposits (fintech promos). Microtask/AI/task platforms are invitation-, profile-, or acceptance-gated with inconsistent volume. The report keeps every claim conservative and never converts per-hit amounts into hourly wages.

## A. UGC / content creation & clipping

### A1. Whop Content Rewards (clipping campaigns)
- **Provider:** Whop, Inc. — official docs: https://docs.whop.com/memberships-and-access/third-party-apps/content-rewards
- **Work:** Join funded campaigns, cut source footage into short clips, post to your TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / X, submit links for approval. Campaign types: clipping, music, logo, UGC.
- **Who pays:** Campaign sponsors (brands/creators) via Whop Payments; Whop auto-pays after approval based on verified views.
- **Payout:** Per 1,000 verified views, rate set per campaign. Live range observed $0.20–$6 per 1,000 views, averaging ~$1 (e.g., one flagged campaign at $2/1k with $10K budget). Some campaigns add flat bonuses (e.g., ~$10 per approved video). Budgets are finite and first-come-first-served.
- **Timing:** Paid after Whop verifies views over the campaign tracking window, once the brand approves the submission.
- **Time required:** Editing time per clip (manual with CapCut/Premiere or AI tools like Opus Clip); clips are unpaid until approved and earning views.
- **Eligibility:** Free Whop account; posting accounts on allowed platforms linked to Whop; no follower/experience requirement stated.
- **Current evidence:** Official docs live and crawled ~Aug 2026 (content-rewards page); multiple 2026 third-party tutorials describe active campaigns with live budgets.
- **Caveat:** Performance-based — a clip with no views earns $0; submissions can be rejected; campaigns can exhaust budgets before your views pay. Licensed content removes copyright risk. Do NOT present as guaranteed income.

### A2. VYRO (campaign clipping)
- **Provider:** VYRO — official help center: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments (fetched 2026-09-23)
- **Work:** Edit provided source material into short clips, post on your social accounts, submit to a campaign.
- **Who pays:** Campaign sponsors/brands; payouts based on eligible views.
- **Payout:** Campaign-dependent; per-clip maximum commonly capped around $500–$2,000. Each approved post must reach at least 5,000 eligible views before it earns.
- **Timing:** Earnings generally available ~7–10 days after the campaign ends/review completes. PayPal or Stripe depending on region; $10 minimum cash-out.
- **Time required:** Editing + posting per clip; unpaid until views threshold + approval.
- **Eligibility:** Open to ordinary users; requires editing ability and a social account.
- **Current evidence:** Official help center current as of 2026-09-23 fetch.
- **Caveat:** Probable — a clip can earn $0 if it misses 5,000 views, is rejected for fraudulent views, or the campaign budget is exhausted. Not same-day. Never describe as guaranteed.

### A3. Vocal Media (writing for reads)
- **Provider:** Vocal (Jerrick Media Holdings) — official: https://vocal.media/resources/how-do-i-get-paid (and Step 8 payout guide, crawled days ago)
- **Work:** Write and publish stories (fiction, essays, reviews, op-eds) on the platform; promote them yourself.
- **Who pays:** Vocal pays per read; readers can also send direct tips via Stripe.
- **Payout:** Free creators: $3.80 per 1,000 reads. Vocal+ (paid tier $9.99/mo — exclude Vocal+ for catalog) $6.00/1,000 reads. Tips go straight to your Stripe minus a 7% transfer fee (free tier).
- **Timing:** Withdrawals at $35 minimum (free tier; $20 Vocal+). Official FAQ: 10–20 days for withdrawal to move to Stripe and show in bank account. Stripe only — no PayPal.
- **Time required:** Writing time per story; entirely unpaid until reads accrue.
- **Eligibility:** Anyone 13+, worldwide, free account.
- **Current evidence:** Official help docs current; rates stated consistently across official pages.
- **Caveat:** Reads (not views) count — only completed reads. This is NOT quick income; most stories earn $0. Tips and Top Story bonuses exist but are unpredictable. Challenges (writing contests) pay prizes — exclude contests per never-contests rule.

## B. Fintech signup promotions (no credit cards)

### B1. moomoo welcome promotion (US)
- **Provider:** moomoo (Moomoo Financial Inc.) — official site: https://www.moomoo.com (promo terms verified via finder.com/doctorofcredit/thewaystowealth, all updated Sept 2026)
- **Work:** Open a new brokerage account through a promotional link; make qualifying net cash deposit (ACH/wire, not ACATS transfers).
- **Who pays:** moomoo.
- **Payout (Sept 2026 verified tiers):** $500 net deposit → $30 in NVDA stock; $2,000 → $100; $10,000 → $200; $50,000 → $400; $100,000 → $1,000. Must maintain average assets ≥ tier for 60 days (120/180 days at top tiers). Separately: 8.1% APY cash-sweep booster on up to $20,000 for 2 months for new customers (runs through Dec 31, 2026).
- **Timing:** Stock reward after meeting deposit + maintenance terms; this is NOT same-day.
- **Time required:** Account opening + KYC (identity verification — ordinary adults only; ~30–60 min).
- **Eligibility:** New customers; 18+; US person typically (SSN/TIN + ID verification). Requires moving $500+ of your own money in for 60 days.
- **Current evidence:** Three independent promo trackers updated within the last 2 weeks (Sept 2026) with matching tier details; deposit deadline for current window Oct 15, 2026 for the tracked variant.
- **Caveat:** This is a capital-locked promo, not earned income — you must park $500 for 60 days to get $30 in stock. Treat as **Probable** (pays per published terms if conditions met; terms change between windows — re-verify live terms before adding). Never present the $1,000 headline without the $100,000 deposit requirement.

### B2. SoFi Invest free-stock sign-up (US)
- **Provider:** SoFi — official site: https://www.sofi.com (terms verified via thewaystowealth.com, updated 8 days ago)
- **Work:** Open SoFi Invest Active Investing account; fund with at least $50 within 45 days; play the virtual "claw machine" for a free stock.
- **Who pays:** SoFi.
- **Payout:** 1 stock worth $5 to $1,000. Published odds: $5 (85.5%), $10 (9.5%), $25 (3.3%), $100 (1.7%), $1,000 (0.028%). Credited within 7 days of meeting requirements. IRA and Auto/Robo accounts NOT eligible.
- **Timing:** Within 7 days after the $50 funding requirement is met.
- **Time required:** Account opening + KYC (~30 min).
- **Eligibility:** New SoFi Invest customers; 18+; US.
- **Current evidence:** Promo tracker updated Sept 2026.
- **Caveat:** Effectively guaranteed ≥$5 for a $50 deposit — expected value ~$6.40. The $1,000 headline is a 1-in-3,500 lottery outcome; present only the odds table, never the headline. Probable.

## C. Online microtasks

### C1. Attapoll (surveys)
- **Provider:** AttaPoll (Hopewiser, UK) — official: https://attapoll.app (**URL not verbatim-verified from an official page fetch — verify before publishing**)
- **Work:** Complete surveys on the mobile app; earnings shown before starting each survey; also app/game milestone tasks.
- **Who pays:** Survey researchers via Attapoll.
- **Payout:** Surveys $0.10–$10 each; realistic ~$1–$2/hour. Withdrawal: $3 minimum for PayPal; $2.50 for Revolut. PayPal withdrawals typically within minutes.

- **Time required:** Phone only; work in dead time. Screen-outs are common — unpaid time is the main hidden cost.
- **Eligibility:** 13+; 70 countries supported; smartphone required.
- **Current evidence:** Multiple Sept 2026 reviews with consistent $3 threshold + instant PayPal details; one personal payment proof (instant PayPal).
- **Caveat:** Volume-gated — survey availability depends on profile/region; frequent disqualifications. Probable per hit. This is the fastest-to-first-cash route on this list.

### C2. Honeygain (passive bandwidth sharing)
- **Provider:** Honeygain — official: https://honeygain.com (**verify payout terms on official site before publishing**)
- **Work:** Install the app; it routes business-intelligence traffic through your unused internet bandwidth. Nothing to do after setup.
- **Who pays:** Honeygain's business customers via Honeygain.
- **Payout:** $20 PayPal minimum. Earnings depend on demand/location; realistically months to a year for a single device to reach $20.
- **Timing:** Months of passive accrual before first cash-out.

- **Eligibility:** Anyone with an internet connection; app required.

- **Caveat:** NOT quick income — include only under a passive/slow lane with explicit "expect months" framing. Also carries privacy/IP-traffic considerations; crypto mode does not satisfy the cash-only rule. Probable.

## D. AI training / data labeling

### D1. Toloka (AI microtasks)
- **Provider:** Toloka — official performer site: https://we.toloka.ai/ (verbatim in multiple current results)
- **Work:** Image labeling/checking, audio evaluation, search-result judging, text classification, content tasks. Training/qualification exams common; accuracy scores gate higher-paying tasks.
- **Who pays:** Task requesters via Toloka.
- **Payout:** Per-task micro amounts ($0.01–$0.20 per page reported by workers); one long-term user estimated ~$20–$25 for 60 hours of beginner work. Realistic beginner rates are $1–$3/hour.
- **Timing:** Paid after task review/approval; withdrawal via PayPal/Payoneer/Skrill (varies by country) with minimums and fees per method.
- **Time required:** Any schedule; high unpaid overhead in training exams and low accuracy-gated access early on.
- **Eligibility:** Global (150+ countries reported); requires language profile setup; some tasks require exams.
- **Current evidence:** Active 2026 community content; we.toloka.ai live.
- **Caveat:** Low pay per hour; task availability inconsistent. Honest framing: spare change for dead time, not income. Probable per task. Current official contributor-payment page not yet fetched — fetch before adding.

### D2. Neevo (AI data tasks)
- **Provider:** Neevo by Defined.ai — official: https://www.neevo.ai/ (verbatim in current results; also neevo.definedcrowd.com)
- **Work:** Speech recording, transcription, text validation, image annotation, audio correction, data validation.
- **Who pays:** Enterprise clients via Defined.ai.
- **Payout:** Per-task (historical reports $0.02–$0.08 per simple task — do NOT treat as current official rate); payment via PayPal after quality review. One user reported $406 for 12 days on a large correction project (~$0.05/task, invitation-only).
- **Timing:** Paid after verification/approval; large projects may have bonus payouts ~45 days later.
- **Time required:** Unpredictable — users report waiting ~2 months with no tasks, then project invitations by email.
- **Eligibility:** Profile/language/skill matching; some tasks require language tests.
- **Current evidence:** 2026 review/tutorial content active; company active.
- **Caveat:** Invitation-gated and project-volume varies heavily by language/country. Probable per accepted project. Current official contributor-payment page not yet fetched — fetch before adding.

### D3. OneForma (AI data projects)
- **Provider:** OneForma (Centific; formerly Pactera EDGE) — official: https://www.oneforma.com
- **Work:** Data annotation, transcription, translation, voice data, AI evaluation, search relevance, usability testing.
- **Who pays:** Enterprise clients via OneForma.
- **Payout:** Per project; observed ranges: annotation $3–$18/hr, testing/linguistic $15–$25/hr (2026 third-party reports — treat as indicative, not official). Paid twice monthly (around the 10th and 25th) after invoice confirmation; $10 minimum per cycle; PayPal available only under $300/year, otherwise Payoneer/bank via Tipalti.
- **Timing:** 2x monthly after approval + invoice confirmation.
- **Time required:** Free to join, but each project has a 5-stage funnel: application → surveys → certifications → signature → recruiter review. One 2026 account took 2+ months to clear certification.
- **Eligibility:** Global; qualification exams required; not all projects accept beginners.
- **Current evidence:** Detailed Aug 2026 review with real account; payment guidelines document.
- **Caveat:** Slow onboarding — not quick income in the near term. Include only under an AI-project lane with the funnel time stated. Probable per project.

### D4. CloudResearch Connect (research studies)
- **Provider:** CloudResearch — official participant page: https://www.cloudresearch.com/products/connect-for-participants/ (fetched in search; content current, last updated ~13 days ago)
- **Work:** Academic/corporate/government research surveys, product studies, diary studies, interviews.
- **Who pays:** Researchers; CloudResearch enforces minimum pay standards.
- **Payout:** Official: researchers required to pay ≥$6/hour, recommended ≥$7.50/hr; average participant earnings $9–10/hr. PayPal or bank transfer (Amazon gift cards also offered — cash via PayPal/bank only for our rule).
- **Timing:** Paid after study approval; withdrawal via payout center.
- **Time required:** Per-study; dashboard shows pay and duration upfront.
- **Eligibility:** Vetted participant pool — screening required; US-focused but open more broadly. Must pass data-quality screening (not everyone accepted).
- **Current evidence:** Official pages current; OpenAI ran a 981-participant 28-day study on Connect (official case study).
- **Caveat:** Study availability depends on demographics; vetting means some users never get in. Probable per study. Strongest "honest survey" option vs. low-rate survey mills.

## E. User / game testing (fixed pay per accepted test)

### E1. Trymata (formerly TryMyUI; now part of QuestionPro)
- **Provider:** Trymata — official tester FAQ: https://trymata.com/learn/tester-faq/ (verbatim from search results)
- **Work:** Record screen + voice while testing websites/apps; answer post-test questions; occasional moderated sessions.
- **Who pays:** Companies running UX tests, via Trymata.
- **Payout:** $5–$30 per test (typical unmoderated 15–25 min ≈ $5–$10; 60-min moderated ≈ $30).
- **Timing:** Official FAQ: payments sent every weekday morning via PayPal, usually within a few days of test acceptance (after review-team approval).
- **Time required:** Qualification test first (unpaid); then invitations matched to your profile.
- **Eligibility:** 18+, computer/smartphone + internet, microphone, PayPal, clear spoken English.
- **Current evidence:** Official FAQ live; 2026 reviewer completed the full tester flow (16-min test); announced merger with QuestionPro (Feb 2026) — tester flow now runs through QuestionPro UX.
- **Caveat:** Invitation-gated; a few tests per week at best; tests can be rejected. Probable per test. Confirm payment method in account during the QuestionPro transition before adding.

### E2. TestingTime (product/user research)
- **Provider:** TestingTime — official: https://www.testingtime.com/en/get-paid-to-test/ and https://www.testingtime.com/en/paid-product-tester/ (fetched in search)
- **Work:** Usability studies, interviews, product tests — remote via Skype/Zoom or in-person (catalog: online only, use remote).
- **Who pays:** Companies (client list includes Microsoft, Google, UBS, Zalando per 2026 review).
- **Payout:** Up to €50/hour; studies typically 30–90 min, paying €20–€200 per study (third-party reports — official states amount shown upfront per study).
- **Timing:** Official: payment via PayPal or bank transfer within 10 business days after completing the study session.
- **Time required:** 5-min demographic questionnaire to start; invitations depend on profile match; spots fill fast.
- **Eligibility:** No experience needed; everyday people. Stronger in Europe (Switzerland, Germany, France, Austria, UK, Netherlands).
- **Current evidence:** Official FAQ pages current (crawled hours ago); 2026 review with Trustpilot 4.0/1,200+ reviews.
- **Caveat:** Invitation-gated; infrequent invites. Probable per study.
