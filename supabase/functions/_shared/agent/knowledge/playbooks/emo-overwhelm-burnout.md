## Principles
- When everything feels too much, shrink the plan. One list, three actions a day, and rest.
- Burnout from a long search is common; rest is part of the plan.
- Support from another person helps more than willpower.
## Steps
- Write everything on one list and pick the three that matter this week.
- Cut the rest or schedule it later.
- Block fixed hours for the search and fixed hours off.
- Share the list with a friend, a mentor or a counselor; ask for a weekly check-in.
- Celebrate small finishes.
- If sleep, mood or health are suffering, say who can help, like a doctor or school counseling.
## Ask
- Ask what the three most urgent things are.
## Do not claim
- Do not give medical advice or promise things will get easier on a timeline.
