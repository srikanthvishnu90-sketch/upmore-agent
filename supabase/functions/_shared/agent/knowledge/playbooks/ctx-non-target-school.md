## Principles
- Employers who recruit from a short list of schools are not the only employers. The route from a school outside that list runs through proof, people and employers that do not use school lists.
- Smaller schools often have closer professors and alumni who answer, which is a real asset.
- The resume has to answer the school question with something better: a project, a result, a recommender.
## Steps
- Pick targets that hire by skill: small and mid-size employers, startups, labs, public sector, and local branches of large employers.
- Build one or two pieces of work that employers can open and judge in two minutes.
- Find alumni of the school in the field; the school's alumni database and LinkedIn search both work. Ask each for fifteen minutes.
- Ask professors to introduce the person; a professor's note is worth more than a form.
- For large recruiters with fixed lists, still apply, but expect to need a referral to be seen.
- Attend or watch events open to all schools, and follow up with each person met within a day.
## Ask
- Ask what field and what proof they have so far.
## Do not claim
- Do not say the school does not matter, or that it does.
