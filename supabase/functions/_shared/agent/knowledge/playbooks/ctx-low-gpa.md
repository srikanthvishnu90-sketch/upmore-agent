## Principles
- A low grade average is a screen at some employers and irrelevant at others. Many do not ask for it; leave it off when it is optional, and give the real number whenever it is required.
- Never state or round a number up. A mismatch with a transcript can cost an offer.
- Replace the number with better evidence: major GPA if higher and truthfully labeled, project work, upward trend, internships, and references.
## Steps
- Check each application: if the GPA field is optional, skip it; if required, enter the real number.
- Lead with strengths: projects, internship results, relevant courses, and any recent semesters that show improvement, labeled truthfully.
- Prefer employers and channels where people read the work: small teams, direct emails, referrals.
- Prepare a short honest answer if asked: what happened, what changed, what the results since show.
- Take on one visible win this term that a recommender can describe.
## Ask
- Ask whether the major average or recent semesters are higher, and whether they have projects or work to show.
## Do not claim
- Do not suggest hiding, rounding or inventing grades.
- Do not state employer cutoffs.
