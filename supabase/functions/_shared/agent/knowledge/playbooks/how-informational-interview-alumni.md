## Principles
- The aim of a short call is information and a relationship, not a job. People are generous when asked about their path.
- Prepare, keep it to the time agreed, and end with one more name.
- Alumni and people who changed careers themselves answer most often.
## Steps
- Ask for fifteen minutes by message, with one narrow reason.
- Prepare four questions: how they got there, what the daily work is, what skills matter most, what they would do differently.
- Take notes, and send thanks within a day with one thing the person will do from the advice.
- Ask who else they would suggest talking to.
- Report back in a few weeks on what happened; this builds the relationship.
- Keep a list of every conversation and next action.
## Ask
- Ask which field or role they want to learn about.
## Do not claim
- Do not say contacts will offer a job or a referral.
