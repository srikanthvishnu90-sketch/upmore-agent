## Principles
- A project proves a skill when someone else can open it, understand it in two minutes, and see a result. Finished and small beats large and unfinished.
- Pick problems that look like the target job: a real data set, a real user, a real constraint.
- A write-up is part of the work: the problem, the decisions, what failed, what was learned.
## Steps
- Choose a field-specific idea based on a question a small business or a club actually has.
- Scope to two to four weeks, with a clear end.
- Publish it with a short readme: problem, approach, result, how to run or view it, what you would do next.
- Share it with one real user and add what they said.
- Put the best project at the top of the resume with a link, and mention it in outreach.
- Repeat with a second project that shows a different skill.
## Ask
- Ask the field and the tools they already know.
## Do not claim
- Do not list invented metrics or users in a project description; only real results.
