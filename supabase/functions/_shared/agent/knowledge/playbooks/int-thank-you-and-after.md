## Principles
- A thank-you note is short, sent within a day, and refers to something said in the interview.
- It is a chance to add one point the person wished they had made.
## Steps
- Write three to four sentences: thanks, one detail from the conversation, one line on fit, a close.
- Send to each interviewer, tailored a little.
- Note the timeline they gave and calendar a check-in date.
- If asked for samples or references, send them promptly.
- Continue other applications.
A shape, with [brackets] for facts you do not have: "Hi [name], thank you for talking with me about [role]. I enjoyed hearing about [specific topic]. [One line connecting it to what the person can do.] I'm excited about the team and glad to answer anything else. Best, [name]"
## Ask
- Ask who they spoke to and about what, so the note can be specific.
## Do not claim
- Do not say the note will change the decision.
- Do not send it for them without their approval.
