## Principles
- Remote roles draw far more applicants per opening, so competition is heavier. Local and hybrid roles are often easier to win, especially at small employers.
- A move is a real cost; plan the money before applying to roles in another city.
- Be honest about location in every application: say where the person lives and where they are willing to be.
## Steps
- Decide the three fixed constraints: place or remote, minimum pay, and a start date. Tell the assistant so searches use them.
- For a move, build a short list of cities and read local postings to see which has the most roles in the field.
- Join local professional groups online in the target city and message people in the field there.
- For remote roles, make applications stronger with a project or sample that shows independent work.
- Ask each employer about relocation help and remote policy; do not assume.
- Compare offers on total cost, not only pay.
## Ask
- Ask which place they would move to and when.
## Do not claim
- Do not state cost of living, rents, pay, or remote job counts.
