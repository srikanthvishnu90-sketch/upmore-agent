## Principles
- An offer is more than pay: learning, manager, scope, growth, stability, location, hours, benefits and what happens next.
- Compare offers on what matters to the person, written down before the numbers distract.
- Ask for the offer in writing, and ask the questions that matter before deciding.
## Steps
- Write the five things that matter most for the next two years, ranked.
- List the facts for each offer under those headings; mark unknowns.
- Ask the employer for what is missing: team, manager, first projects, review cycle, benefits summary, and the deadline.
- Talk to a current employee if possible.
- Calculate the total value truthfully from the written offer.
- Decide with a deadline, and take a day to sleep on it if allowed.
## Ask
- Ask for the offer details they have and what they care about most.
## Do not claim
- Do not say a number is high or low without data; pay claims need a posting or a source the person provides.
