# Depth protocol: INV-011, buy a stock or ETF from a text (real-world C46)

Owner: Claude. Status words: planned, built, proven (its test fails under the planted break and passes without it).

Why third: of the 206 real-world capabilities, this is the first write where a wrong word costs real money with no undo: a market order fills in seconds, and a fill on the wrong instrument, the wrong size or a stale price is a realized loss before the receipt arrives. It is also the reference example the real-world set names as the depth bar ("invest from a message").

Scope: the user says "buy $500 of VOO" (or "buy 2 shares of VOO") and a real order is placed at the connected brokerage, after explicit approval of the exact order, and either fills (price, shares, time, total) or is queued outside market hours with an order id. User-directed orders only: any decision word ("should I", "whatever's best", "the dip") is refused by the advice guard. Execution stays closed by the compliance gate until a broker-dealer partner is live; the logic is proven against a fake rail that behaves like one. Sells (INV-012), recurring buys (INV-013), limit and stop orders (C57, C58) and crypto (C56) are out of scope except where this capability must refuse to be used for them.

Files: `packages/domain/66-agent-trade.js` (the dialogue and state machine over AgentInvest, AgentLoop, AdviceGuard, Compliance, AgentVoice and RealWorld), `tests/depth-INV-011.test.cjs` (one test per sub-capability, tagged `[S01]` to `[S15]`, plus the adversarial log `[ADV]`), `evals/depth/INV-011.mutations.cjs` (one planted defect per sub-capability).

Check: `node --test tests/depth-INV-011.test.cjs && node evals/depth/run.cjs INV-011`

## The 15 sub-capabilities (enumerated before implementation)

| # | Sub-capability | What the user sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S01 | Dollars or shares parse exactly | "$500 of VOO", "500 dollars of VOO", "VOO $500", "2 shares of VOO", "buy 1.5 VOO" become the exact notional in cents or the exact share count; "buy VOO" asks "How much: dollars or shares?"; "all my cash", "a bunch" and "0" ask instead of guessing. |
| S02 | The symbol resolves against real instruments | "VOO" resolves to the listed instrument with its name; "vanguard s&p 500" by name asks "Did you mean VOO, Vanguard S&P 500 ETF?"; an unknown ticker asks and never guesses; two matches ask which; a symbol that is not tradable at the account (OTC, crypto) says so. |
| S03 | Decision words are refused, direction is obeyed | "should I buy VOO", "buy whatever's best", "buy the stock that just dipped" get the advice-guard redirect and nothing is staged; "buy $500 of VOO" is user-directed and proceeds. |
| S04 | A real quote prices the estimate, or nothing does | The estimate is shares x price from a quote with source and as-of; a quote older than the freshness window or missing asks to refresh and shows no number; dollars convert to shares at 4 decimals when the account supports fractions, else whole shares with the leftover named. |
| S05 | The account is chosen, never assumed | One linked brokerage is used and named; several ask "Which account: Schwab brokerage or Fidelity Roth?"; a named account that is not linked asks; an account that cannot trade (view-only) says so. |
| S06 | Buying power is read before asking for a yes | Settled cash is read from the account with its as-of; a shortfall is stated to the cent ("$320.00 settled; $500.00 needs $180.00 more") and nothing is staged; enough cash proceeds. |
| S07 | The confirmation restates the exact order | Side, dollars or shares, symbol with name, estimated total at the quoted price with source and as-of, account, order type, when it will execute (now, or queued until the 9:30 ET open), and "reply yes or no". A money confirmation without amount and account is refused by the loop. |
| S08 | No partner, no order | Without a live broker-dealer partner the compliance gate blocks with "I can't place orders yet" and nothing is staged or written; with the partner live the same request confirms. |
| S09 | Nothing before yes; no is nothing; a stale yes is nothing | The rail sees no write before approval; "no" writes nothing; a yes after the confirmation expires (10 minutes) is refused and the order is re-quoted on the next ask. |
| S10 | "Yes but" corrects and re-quotes | "yes but make it $300" drops the old proposal, re-stages with a fresh quote and asks again; nothing is written in between. |
| S11 | Market closed means queued, said plainly | Outside 9:30 to 16:00 ET on a trading day the confirmation says it will queue until the open; after yes the receipt says queued with the order id, never filled; the follow-up reads the fill when the broker reports it. |
| S12 | A fill is read back, not assumed | The receipt carries fill price, shares, time and total from the broker's record; the position appears in the holdings read; a partial fill is reported as partial with the remainder; the real-world proof (C46) reads done from the same record. |
| S13 | The same order is not placed twice | The same instruction twice in a day answers "already placed (order id)" and writes nothing; "another $500 of VOO" is a second order with its own id. |
| S14 | Rail failure is unknown or failed, never done | A write that throws is unknown: no retry, a follow-up reconciles by order id; a broker rejection (insufficient buying power, symbol halted) is failed with the broker's reason and "nothing was placed". |
| S15 | The receipt and the memory tell the truth | The receipt names order id, filled or queued, total, source and as-of; the memory outcome records the amount; every outgoing message passes the advice-guard response lint; "what did you buy today" lists the fills from memory. |

## The three worst failures with real money or trust, and what guards each

1. **The wrong order fills.** Wrong instrument (name confusion), wrong size (dollars read as shares), wrong price (stale quote), wrong account. Guards: S01, S02, S04, S05, S07.
2. **An order the user did not approve, approved twice, or approved stale.** A decision made for the user, an order with no partner, a write before the yes, a duplicate text, a yes ten minutes late. Guards: S03, S06, S08, S09, S10, S13.
3. **The agent says filled when it is not.** Queued reported as filled, a partial as full, a rejection or a timeout as done. Guards: S11, S12, S14, S15.
